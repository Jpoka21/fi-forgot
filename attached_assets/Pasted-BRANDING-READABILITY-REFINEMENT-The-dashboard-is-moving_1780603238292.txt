BRANDING + READABILITY REFINEMENT

The dashboard is moving in the right direction, but we have identified two major issues:

1. The dashboard still feels slightly too much like a software dashboard.
2. Some text has become difficult to read due to low contrast and small typography.

We want to move the experience closer to the F*I Forgot brand.

BRAND DIRECTION

F*I Forgot is not:

* Relationship therapy
* Self improvement software
* A health tracker
* A corporate CRM

F*I Forgot is:

* Thoughtful
* Helpful
* Slightly playful
* Reassuring
* Human

The core promise is:

"We got your back."

Users are paying because they do not want to forget important people and important moments.

The dashboard should reinforce that feeling.

PRIMARY BRAND MESSAGE

Move away from making Relationship Health the emotional centerpiece.

People and moments are the centerpiece.

Use language such as:

We Got Your Back

You're Covered

On Track

Card Ready

No Important Moments At Risk

Everything Important Is Being Watched

COMING UP SECTION

The most important content on the dashboard should be:

People and upcoming moments.

The hero should focus on:

Stacy
Birthday in 20 days
Card draft ready

Not:

59%

People first.
Metrics second.

BROWNIE POINTS

Rename Relationship Health to:

Brownie Points

Reason:

* More memorable
* More on brand
* More playful
* Easier to understand
* Fits existing pricing language

Examples:

Brownie Points

59

Building Momentum

Your cards are getting more personal every month.

A few more details about Stacy and Mom will make future cards feel even more thoughtful.

Staying Out Of Trouble

Legend Status

Cap displayed score at 98.

WE GOT YOUR BACK SECTION

Near the top of the dashboard, include a reassuring section.

Example:

We Got Your Back

✓ Stacy's birthday is on track

✓ 2 cards ready for review

✓ No important moments at risk

✓ 6 occasions being watched

This should feel like a concierge update.

PEOPLE YOU MAY BE FORGETTING

Add More People

Examples:

Dad

Close Friend

Brother

Coworker

This feels much more on brand.

READABILITY IMPROVEMENTS

This is extremely important.

Several sections currently have low contrast text that is difficult to read.

Improve readability throughout the dashboard.

Use stronger contrast.

Typography should always prioritize readability over style.

TEXT COLORS

Primary text:

#1F1F1F

Secondary text:

#4B5563

Avoid using very light gray text on cream backgrounds.

Avoid low contrast labels.

Avoid tiny light colored descriptions.

All informational text should pass accessibility contrast standards.

FONT SIZES

Increase readability of:

* descriptions
* helper text
* score explanations
* status labels
* relationship summaries

Avoid tiny text.

If text feels decorative rather than readable, increase size and contrast.

HEADINGS

Keep the F*I Forgot brand font for:

* major headings
* section labels
* branding

Use a clean modern font such as Inter or Geist for:

* body copy
* descriptions
* statuses
* helper text
* relationship details

SPACING

Increase vertical spacing between sections.

Allow more breathing room.

Avoid cramped layouts.

The page should feel relaxed and easy to scan.

BUTTONS

Red buttons should remain the primary CTA.

However:

Do not allow buttons to visually overpower people and moments.

The user's eye should first notice:

* Stacy
* Mom
* Dad
* Upcoming moments

Before noticing:

* buttons
* scores
* metrics

SUCCESS CRITERIA

When a user opens the dashboard, their reaction should be:

"Good. We got Stacy covered."

Not:

"Let me analyze my metrics."

The dashboard should feel like a relationship concierge that helps users stay thoughtful and avoid forgetting important people.

People are the hero.

Moments are the story.

Brownie Points are the supporting score.
