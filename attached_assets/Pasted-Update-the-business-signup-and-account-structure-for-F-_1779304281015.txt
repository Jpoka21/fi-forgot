Update the business signup and account structure for F* I Forgot.

We need one shared login system, but separate personal and business workspaces.

Do not create separate login accounts for personal and business.

Rules:

1. A user account should be the main login identity.
   Example:
   email
   password
   full name

2. A user can have one or more workspaces.
   Workspace types:
   personal
   business

3. Personal and business dashboards must stay separate.
   Same login, different dashboards.

4. If a new user signs up from the business signup page:
   Create the user account.
   Then create their first business workspace.
   Save business name and business type on the business workspace, not only on the user.

5. If an existing personal user clicks “Start remembering clients” or signs in from the business page:
   Do not make them create a second account.
   After login, send them to a “Create your business workspace” page.
   Ask for:
   business name
   business type
   optional business address, if easy
   Then create a business workspace tied to their existing user account.

6. If a user has both personal and business workspaces:
   Show a workspace switcher after login or in the dashboard header.
   Options:
   Personal
   Business Name

7. Business signup page changes:
   Keep the current design.
   Keep fields:
   Full Name
   Business Name
   Email
   Password
   Business Type
   Start Remembering Clients
   But make sure business name gets saved to the business workspace.

8. Dashboard routing:
   personal workspace goes to personal dashboard
   business workspace goes to business dashboard

9. Add a simple database structure if needed:
   users table:
   id
   full_name
   email
   password_hash
   created_at

   workspaces table:
   id
   user_id
   type
   name
   business_type
   business_address
   created_at

10. Make sure existing users without a business workspace can add one later from the business page.

Goal:
One email and password per person.
Separate personal and business experiences.
No duplicate accounts.
Business name belongs to the business workspace.
