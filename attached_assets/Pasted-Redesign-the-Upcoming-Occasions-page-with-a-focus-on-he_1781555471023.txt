Redesign the Upcoming Occasions page with a focus on helping users quickly understand what needs attention and what is already handled.

The goal is not simply to make the page prettier.

The goal is to make the page feel like a personal assistant that tells users exactly what needs action.

Keep the page highly scannable and efficient even with 20–50 upcoming occasions.

CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Prioritize action over information.

A user should immediately understand:

1. Who the person is
2. What occasion is coming
3. How soon it is
4. Whether anything needs to be done

IMPORTANT

Do not turn this into large stacked cards.

Do not dramatically increase row height.

Keep the experience compact and easy to scan.

We are optimizing for speed and clarity, not visual decoration.

--------------------------------------------------
1. Replace Emoji Avatars
--------------------------------------------------

Remove the current emoji avatar squares.

Replace them with circular recipient badges containing initials.

Examples:

JM
ER
DS

Use subtle brand colors.

Future profile photo support should remain possible.

--------------------------------------------------
2. Add Relationship Context
--------------------------------------------------

Display relationship information near the recipient name.

Examples:

ERIC
Brother

SARAH
Mom

ALEX
Son

Use muted styling.

Keep it visually secondary.

If relationship data is missing, do not show an empty placeholder.

--------------------------------------------------
3. Improve Occasion Visibility
--------------------------------------------------

Increase prominence of:

• Occasion type
• Occasion date

Current occasion information is too easy to miss.

Users should instantly recognize:

Birthday
Anniversary
Father's Day
Holiday
Work Anniversary

Make the date easier to scan as well.

--------------------------------------------------
4. Make Urgency More Obvious
--------------------------------------------------

Days remaining should be one of the strongest visual elements on the row.

Examples:

2 DAYS
6 DAYS
15 DAYS

Apply urgency states:

Red:
0–7 days

Orange:
8–14 days

Neutral:
15+ days

Urgency should draw attention before users read the row details.

--------------------------------------------------
5. Add Action Status Hierarchy
--------------------------------------------------

This is the most important change.

Rows should clearly communicate whether action is needed.

Examples:

🟢 Card Ready
🟡 Needs Review
🔴 Missing Information
🔴 Card Not Started

Users should immediately know what requires attention.

A card due in 2 days without a draft should visually outrank a card due in 15 days that is already ready.

--------------------------------------------------
6. Consider Action First Sorting
--------------------------------------------------

Evaluate whether occasions should be grouped by status before date.

Example:

Needs Action

• Card Not Started
• Missing Information
• Needs Review

Ready To Go

• Card Ready

Within each section, continue sorting by upcoming date.

The goal is reducing the chance a user misses something important.

--------------------------------------------------
7. Improve Row Separation
--------------------------------------------------

Current rows visually blend together.

Increase spacing slightly.

Improve padding.

Add subtle hover states.

Use gentle borders or separators.

Do not dramatically increase row height.

The page should remain compact and easy to scan.

--------------------------------------------------
8. Refine Background Colors
--------------------------------------------------

Do not immediately replace the existing warm theme.

Instead, modernize and refine it.

Explore:

• Slightly lighter warm neutral background
• Better contrast
• Cleaner white cards
• Improved border definition

Avoid making the page look like a generic SaaS dashboard.

Maintain warmth and personality.

--------------------------------------------------
9. Simplify Header Copy
--------------------------------------------------

Replace:

UPCOMING OCCASIONS — WE GOT YOUR BACK

With:

Upcoming Occasions

or

Coming Up Soon

Use calmer typography.

Reduce visual noise.

--------------------------------------------------
10. Reduce Quick Card Visual Weight
--------------------------------------------------

Quick Card is useful but should not dominate the page.

Compress it into a smaller utility section.

It should remain accessible without competing with upcoming occasions.

--------------------------------------------------
11. Improve Navigation Clarity
--------------------------------------------------

The active navigation item should be immediately obvious.

Improve contrast and visual hierarchy between:

• Home
• Your People
• Upcoming Occasions
• Brownie Points

Users should instantly know where they are.

--------------------------------------------------
12. Review Dashboard Metrics
--------------------------------------------------

Evaluate whether the top metrics are helping users make decisions.

Current metrics:

• People
• Upcoming
• Cards Ready
• At Risk

Consider:

• People
• Occasions Coming Up
• Cards Ready
• Need Attention

Do not remove them without evaluating their usefulness.

--------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------

When a user lands on this page they should instantly know:

• Which occasion is most urgent
• Which relationships need attention
• Which cards are already handled
• Which cards can safely be ignored for now

The page should feel less like a spreadsheet and more like a proactive relationship assistant.
