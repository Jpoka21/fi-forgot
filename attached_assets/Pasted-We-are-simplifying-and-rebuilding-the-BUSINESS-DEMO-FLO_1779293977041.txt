The current business demo flow and previous prompts drifted too far into CRM and follow up software territory.

This is NOT a follow up automation platform.

F* I Forgot for Business is about:
REAL GREETING CARDS FOR IMPORTANT CLIENT EVENTS ON AUTOPILOT.

We are simplifying the business experience dramatically.

The business version should feel:
- simple
- scalable
- thoughtful
- lightweight
- easy for businesses with hundreds of clients

NOT:
- emotionally heavy
- complex workflows
