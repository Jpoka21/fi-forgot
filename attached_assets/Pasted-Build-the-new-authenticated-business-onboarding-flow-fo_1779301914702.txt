Build the new authenticated business onboarding flow for the “Start Remembering Clients” button.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT the same flow as the “See How It Works” demo.

The demo flow remains untouched and still uses one question per page.

This new flow is the REAL business product experience and should feel like a modern CRM/dashboard built for speed and bulk client management.

---

## FLOW OVERVIEW

1. User clicks:
   “Start Remembering Clients”

2. User is taken to:
   Sign Up / Create Account page

Fields:

* Full Name
* Business Name
* Email
* Password

“What type of business are you?”

* Real Estate
* Mortgage
* Insurance
* Financial Advisor
* Attorney
* Medical
* Other

3. After signup/login:
   Redirect user to the new Business Dashboard.
