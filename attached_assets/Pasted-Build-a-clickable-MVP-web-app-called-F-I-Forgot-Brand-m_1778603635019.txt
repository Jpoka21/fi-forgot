Build a clickable MVP web app called F.I. Forgot.

Brand meaning:
The name implies “F, I forgot,” but stays funny and safe. Use the spelling F.I. Forgot throughout the app.

Target customer:
Men who forget important card giving dates for the women in their lives.

Core positioning:
A relationship disaster prevention service for husbands, boyfriends, sons, dads, and forgetful men.

Brand promise:
“Never forget the card again.”

MVP goal:
This is a clickable demo only. Do not build real payments, printing, mailing, SMS, or AI integrations yet. Use mock data and placeholder logic.

Brand voice:
Funny, self aware, masculine, warm, and trustworthy.

Important recipient logic:
The app is designed for men sending cards to women.
Recipient types should include:
Wife
Girlfriend
Mom
Mother in law
Daughter
Grandmother
Sister
Other important woman

Delivery logic:
If the recipient is a wife or girlfriend, default delivery should be “Mail it to me so I can hand it to her.”
If the recipient is mom, mother in law, grandmother, sister, daughter, or other, default delivery should be “Mail it directly to her.”
Allow the user to change this.
