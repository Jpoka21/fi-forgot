# FiForgot Dashboard Density Pass

The new dashboard structure is much better.

Do not redesign from scratch.

Do not remove functionality.

This pass is about sizing, spacing, and information density.

---

# Main Problem

The dashboard is too tall for the amount of information shown.

There is too much scrolling.

Large cards are taking up too much vertical space.

The goal is:

Reduce total dashboard height by approximately 40 percent without removing important information.

---

# Above The Fold Goal

On desktop, users should see these three things without major scrolling:

1. We Got Your Back
2. Upcoming Moments
3. Your People

Quick Card should also be visible near the top if possible.

Everything else can be lower.

---

# We Got Your Back

Keep it.

But reduce its height by about 50 percent.

Make it a compact status strip, not a giant hero block.

It should still feel branded and reassuring.

But it should not dominate the first screen.

---

# Upcoming Moments

Keep this as the most important section.

Make the cards more compact.

Each card should show:

Name

Relationship

Occasion

Days remaining

Primary action

Use a tighter layout.

Think:

Boarding pass

Not oversized menu card.

Reduce unnecessary vertical padding.

---

# Your People

Make this section more compact.

Show people as smaller horizontal cards or tiles.

Do not use huge cards for only two pieces of information.

Each person tile should show:

Relationship icon

Name

Relationship

Next occasion badge

---

# Quick Card

Move Quick Card higher if possible.

But make it compact.

It should not be a giant banner.

It should feel like a helpful action tile.

Example:

⚡ Quick Card

Generate a card in under 60 seconds.

Coming Soon

---

# Relationship Insights And Health

Keep them.

But make them lower priority and more compact.

Avoid repeating:

Relationship Insights

Relationship Health

Relationship Insights

Consolidate duplicate labels.

Use one clean secondary section.

---

# Bottom Sections

Recommended Next Step

Plan Usage

Brownie Points

Richest Profile

These should not dominate the dashboard.

Use compact side by side cards where possible.

---

# Visual Rules

Reduce oversized padding.

Reduce card heights.

Keep whitespace, but make it intentional.

Avoid giant cards with one sentence.

Use tighter section spacing.

Use softer borders.

Keep the premium feel, but make it more efficient.

---

# Success Test

A user should not have to scroll through multiple screens just to see basic dashboard information.

The dashboard should feel:

Cleaner

Faster

More useful

Less stretched out

Less empty

More like a product dashboard

Less like a long landing page
