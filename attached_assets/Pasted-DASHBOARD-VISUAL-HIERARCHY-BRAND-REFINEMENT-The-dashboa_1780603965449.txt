DASHBOARD VISUAL HIERARCHY + BRAND REFINEMENT

The dashboard is moving in the right direction, but there is still a major visual hierarchy issue.

The problem is no longer layout.

The problem is visibility.

Several important sections are visually disappearing.

CURRENT ISSUE

The following section titles are too small, too light, and too easy to miss:

* We Got Your Back
* Recommended Next Step
* Brownie Points
* Your Relationships
* People You May Be Forgetting

Currently these feel like metadata labels.

They do not feel like section headlines.

Users notice the content cards before they notice the section titles.

This is backwards.

SECTION HEADINGS MUST BECOME HEADLINES

These are major sections.

Treat them like headlines, not labels.

DESIRED BEHAVIOR

When users scan the page they should immediately see:

We Got Your Back

Recommended Next Step

Brownie Points

Your Relationships

before they read the content underneath.

VISUAL CHANGES

For all major section titles:

* Increase font size significantly
* Use darker color
* Reduce excessive letter spacing
* Increase weight
* Add more spacing above and below
* Make them feel intentional

Use the F*I Forgot brand font for section titles.

Use charcoal or near-black.

Suggested:

#1F1F1F

Do not use light gray.

Do not use tiny text.

Do not use excessive tracking.

TITLE CASE

Move away from ALL CAPS section titles.

Current:

WE GOT YOUR BACK

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP

BROWNIE POINTS

Desired:

We Got Your Back

Recommended Next Step

Brownie Points

This feels more premium, more human, and more aligned with the direction of the dashboard.

The dashboard should feel like a helpful concierge.

Not a control panel.

WE GOT YOUR BACK SECTION

This section represents the core promise of the product.

It should feel important.

Example:

We Got Your Back

Everything important is being watched.

✓ Stacy's birthday is on track

✓ 2 cards ready for review

✓ No important moments at risk

✓ 6 occasions being watched

This section should create relief.

This is one of the most important sections on the page.

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP

This section should feel important and actionable.

The title should stand out.

Users should immediately understand:

"This is what I should do next."

The title should not disappear above the card.

BROWNIE POINTS

The Brownie Points section needs stronger identity.

Current issue:

The score is visible.
The title is not.

Users notice:

59

before noticing:

Brownie Points

The section should clearly communicate:

Brownie Points

59

Building Momentum

Your cards are getting more personal every month.

Keep Brownie Points as a supporting score.

Do not let it become the primary focus of the dashboard.

PEOPLE ARE THE HERO

Continue reinforcing:

People first.
Scores second.

The first thing users should care about:

Stacy
Mom
Dad

The second thing they should care about:

Brownie Points

The score supports the relationship.

The relationship is the product.

DESKTOP LAYOUT IMPROVEMENT

The dashboard still feels too narrow and left-heavy.

Use the available desktop width better.

The page should feel balanced.

Suggested structure:

Left side:

* Upcoming person card
* Relationships

Right side:

* We Got Your Back
* Recommended Next Step
* Brownie Points
* Recent Wins

Avoid making desktop feel like a mobile layout stretched wider.

READABILITY IMPROVEMENTS

Several text elements are still difficult to read.

Increase readability throughout.

Use:

Primary text:
#1F1F1F

Secondary text:
#4B5563

Avoid low contrast gray text on cream backgrounds.

Increase readability for:

* subtitles
* helper text
* section descriptions
* small status labels
* score explanations

If readability and style conflict, choose readability.

BUTTON HIERARCHY

Buttons should not dominate the page.

The eye should notice:

1. Stacy
2. Upcoming moments
3. We Got Your Back
4. Recommended Next Step

before noticing:

Review Cards
View
Add Person

Buttons should support the content, not overpower it.

When a user opens the dashboard:

We Got Your Back

A thoughtful concierge.
