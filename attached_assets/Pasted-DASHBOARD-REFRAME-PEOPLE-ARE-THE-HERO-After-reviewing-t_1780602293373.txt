DASHBOARD REFRAME: PEOPLE ARE THE HERO

After reviewing the latest dashboard iterations, I believe we have identified the core issue.

We have been treating Relationship Health as the hero.

It is not.

The people are the hero.

Relationship Health is important, but it should support the experience rather than lead it.

The reason users pay for F*I Forgot is not to improve a score.

The reason users pay is:

* To remember Stacy's birthday.
* To send thoughtful cards.
* To avoid forgetting important people.
* To strengthen relationships.

The dashboard should reflect that.

NEW PRINCIPLE

When a user logs in, the first thing they should see is:

People and Moments

Not metrics.

Not scores.

Not analytics.

The dashboard should immediately answer:

Who needs me?
What's coming up?
Am I covered?

before it answers:

How is my score doing?

NEW DASHBOARD HIERARCHY

1. Important People & Upcoming Moments
2. You're Covered
3. Recommended Next Step
4. Relationship Health
5. Relationship List
6. Relationship Insights
7. Recent Wins
8. Plan Usage

SECTION 1: IMPORTANT PEOPLE & UPCOMING MOMENTS

This becomes the hero section.

Examples:

Stacy ❤️

Birthday in 20 days

Card draft ready

[Review Card]

---

Mom 🌷

Easter in 34 days

On Track

---

Dad 🎂

Birthday in 55 days

Needs one more memory

[Add Memory]

The hero should feel emotional and instantly understandable.

The user should immediately recognize the people they care about.

The people should be more visually prominent than the scores.

SECTION 2: YOU'RE COVERED

Immediately below the hero.

Example:

You're Covered

✓ No important moments at risk

✓ 2 cards ready for review

✓ 6 occasions being watched

✓ Stacy's birthday is on track

This section should create reassurance.

The user should feel:

"Good. Everything important is being handled."

Do not make this feel like analytics.

Make it feel like a personal concierge update.

SECTION 3: RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP

Keep one recommendation only.

Examples:

Review Stacy's Birthday Card

or

Add one recent memory for Dad

or

Add Mom's favorite restaurant

The recommendation should explain why it matters.

Example:

Add one recent memory for Dad to make future cards more personal.

Not:

Gain 4%.

Relationship outcomes should be more important than score gains.

SECTION 4: RELATIONSHIP HEALTH

Move Relationship Health lower.

Relationship Health should still exist, but it should no longer dominate the page.

Example:

Relationship Health

59%

Building Up

Your cards are becoming more personal.

Add a few more memories and details to make them even stronger.

Trend:

+4% this month

The explanation should be more important than the number.

SECTION 5: RELATIONSHIP LIST

Keep a compact relationship list.

Each person should show:

Name

Human status

Next event

Relationship Health

Action

Example:

Stacy

Strong Profile

Birthday in 20 days

92%

[View]

Example:

Mom

Good Foundation

Easter in 34 days

68%

[Improve]

People should feel like people, not CRM records.

SECTION 6: RELATIONSHIP INSIGHTS

Rename:

"Why Your Score Is What It Is"

This sounds analytical and boring.

Replace with something more human such as:

Make Future Cards Better

or

Help Us Know Them Better

or

Personalize Future Cards

This section should explain:

What information is missing

Why it matters

How it improves future cards

The focus should be card quality and personalization.

Not scoring mechanics.

SECTION 7: RECENT WINS

Lean into positive reinforcement.

Examples:

🎉 Stacy's birthday card is ready

🎉 Mom's profile improved 8%

🎉 No important moments missed in 6 months

Celebrate outcomes.

Not percentages.

This section should make the user feel good.

SECTION 8: PLAN USAGE

Keep visually small.

Keep near the bottom.

Do not let plan usage compete with relationships.

COPY DIRECTION

Use language like:

You're Covered

On Track

Card Ready

Building Up

Strong Profile

Good Foundation

Everything important is being watched

No important moments at risk

Thoughtful cards. Stronger relationships.

Avoid language like:

Coverage Score

Optimization

Performance

Task Completion

Operational Metrics

Utilization

The dashboard should feel human.

VISUAL DIRECTION

The people should visually dominate the page.

The user should see:

Stacy

Mom

Dad

before they focus on:

59%

The relationships are the product.

The score is supporting evidence.

SUCCESS CRITERIA

When a user opens the dashboard, their first thought should be:

"Good. Stacy's birthday is handled."

Not:

"My Relationship Health is 59%."

That emotional shift is the goal of this redesign.
