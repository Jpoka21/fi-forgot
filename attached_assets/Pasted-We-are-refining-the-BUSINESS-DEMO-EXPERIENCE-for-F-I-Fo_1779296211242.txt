We are refining the BUSINESS DEMO EXPERIENCE for F* I Forgot.

IMPORTANT:
The personal side and business side are intentionally different.

The personal side uses deep emotional personalization.

The business side should feel:
- professional
- scalable
- appropriately personal
- intelligent without being creepy
- easy to automate for hundreds of clients

We do NOT want businesses answering deeply personal questions about clients.

However, the current business demo feels too generic because it lacks enough business context to explain WHY a card was selected.

We are fixing that.

-----------------------------------
1. KEEP THE CURRENT SIMPLE BUSINESS EVENT STRUCTURE
-----------------------------------

Continue using the simplified business event model:
- Birthdays
- Holiday / Christmas Cards
- Client Anniversaries
- Home Purchase Anniversaries
- Referral Thank You Cards

Do NOT reintroduce:
- sympathy cards
- new baby cards
- emotional life event automation
- CRM style follow up logic

Keep the business side lightweight and scalable.

-----------------------------------
2. ADD NEW BUSINESS CONTEXT QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------

After the event selection step, add lightweight business context questions.

These questions should help personalize:
- card style
- wording
- message tone
- card selection reasoning

WITHOUT becoming deeply personal.

-----------------------------------
QUESTION:
“What tone fits your business?”

Options:
- Professional
- Friendly
- Warm Professional
- Luxury / High End
- Casual

This selection should affect:
- card design
- message wording
- preview explanation

-----------------------------------
QUESTION:
“What type of relationship is this?”

Options:
- First Time Client
- Repeat Client
- VIP Client
- Referral Partner

This should affect:
- appreciation level
- warmth of wording
- card explanation

-----------------------------------
QUESTION:
“How long has this relationship existed?”

Options:
- New Relationship
- 1 to 3 Years
- Long Term Relationship

This should subtly influence:
- message tone
- appreciation wording
- explanation text

-----------------------------------
3. IMPROVE THE FINAL CARD PREVIEW SECTION
-----------------------------------

The business side MUST still show:
- the card selected
- the message selected
- WHY the system chose it

This is important because this is where the product feels intelligent.

The current “Suggested Holiday Card” section feels too generic.

Replace it with a smarter explanation.

-----------------------------------
4. ADD A “WHY THIS CARD WAS CHOSEN” SECTION
-----------------------------------

Above the card preview add:

Headline:
Why we selected this card

Then dynamically generate explanation text based on the demo answers.

Examples:

Example 1:
“We selected a warm professional holiday card because you chose a long term client relationship and a warm business tone.”

Example 2:
“We selected a clean professional birthday card because this relationship type works best with thoughtful but business appropriate messaging.”

Example 3:
“Referral partners often respond best to appreciation focused cards with a more personal tone.”

This should feel:
- intelligent
- believable
- scalable
- business appropriate

NOT:
overly emotional or creepy.
