We are adding a business side demo flow for F* I Forgot.

Goal:
When a visitor clicks the business hero CTA, do NOT send them to login or signup first. Send them to a free demo flow that explains the business product and asks a few lightweight questions before signup.

Important:
The personal side and business side must stay separate.
Do not change the personal demo flow.
Do not change the personal homepage.
Only update the business side.

Changes to make:

1. Update the business hero CTA
Find the business page hero button that currently says:
“Start Remembering Clients”

Change the click destination so it routes to a new business demo page:
`/business-demo`

Change button text to:
“Demo For Free”

Keep the same visual style as the current red CTA button.

If there is a secondary button that says “See How It Works,” route that to the same `/business-demo` page for now.

2. Create a new route/page
Create a new page at:
`/business-demo`

This page should feel like the business version of the product, not the personal version.

Tone:
Professional, slightly funny, simple, bold, and brand consistent.

Header eyebrow:
F* I FORGOT · FOR BUSINESS

Main headline:
See how many client relationships are quietly going cold.

Subheadline:
Answer a few quick questions and we’ll show how F* I Forgot helps your business remember clients, customers, and referral partners before they drift away.

6. Add final CTA buttons
Primary red button:
“Start Remembering Clients”

This button should route to the existing business signup flow or signup page.

Secondary outline button:
“Back to Business Page”

7. Important UX requirements
Make the page mobile friendly.
Each question should fit comfortably on a phone screen.
Use large buttons for answer choices.
Allow back and next navigation.
Do not require signup before completing the demo.
Do not ask for email in this first version.
Do not store this demo data permanently yet unless the current app already has a safe demo state pattern.
Use local component state only.

8. Visual design
Use the existing F* I Forgot colors:
dark navy background
red CTA buttons
white text
subtle card panels
business professional style

Make it feel connected to the current business hero page.

9. Final check
After implementing:
Clicking “Demo For Free” from the business page should open `/business-demo`.
The visitor should be able to complete all questions without logging in.
Only after the final results screen should they be asked to start/signup.
