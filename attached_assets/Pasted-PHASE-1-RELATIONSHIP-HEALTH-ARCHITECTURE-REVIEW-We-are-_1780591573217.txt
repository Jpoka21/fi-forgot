PHASE 1: RELATIONSHIP HEALTH ARCHITECTURE REVIEW

We are redesigning the F*I Forgot personal dashboard around a new concept called Relationship Health.

IMPORTANT:
Do not implement anything yet.

I want you to audit the existing codebase first and return a detailed proposal before making any changes.

GOALS

The product should feel less like a card sending dashboard and more like a relationship health platform.

The emotional goal is:

1. The user feels reassured that important people and moments are covered.
2. The user understands what to improve next.
3. The user can see measurable progress over time.

The card is the output.
The relationship profile is the asset.

NEW CORE CONCEPTS

Relationship Health

Measures:

* How well the system knows each person.
* How prepared the system is to create meaningful cards and reminders.

Relationship Coverage

Measures:

* Whether important people and important moments are being watched and covered.

These should remain separate concepts.
