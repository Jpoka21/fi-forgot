Sprint 5: Card Quality & Personalization

Goal:

Make generated cards feel like they were written by the sender, not by AI.

Before implementing changes, audit the current card generation pipeline and identify the biggest remaining causes of generic sounding cards.

Focus on the following:

1. Memory Weaving

Today, cards may reference individual memories.

Improve generation so multiple memories are naturally connected together.

Example:

Weak:
“Congratulations on your promotion. I also remember our Disney trip.”

Strong:
“Seeing you earn that promotion reminded me of our Disney trip, when you spent the entire week pushing through every challenge with the same determination.”

The card should feel like one story, not a list of facts.

2. Relationship Voice

Strengthen differences between:

* Spouse
* Parent
* Child
* Friend
* Sibling
* Coworker

A spouse card should not sound like a friend card with stronger adjectives.

Each relationship should have distinct emotional patterns, structure, and tone.

3. Fresh Update Utilization

Audit whether fresh updates are actually making it into final card content.

If fresh updates exist, they should heavily influence the card.

Recent memories should generally matter more than profile facts.

4. Generic Language Detection

Identify and reduce phrases that sound AI generated.

Examples:

* “Wishing you all the best”
* “Hope your special day is filled with joy”
* “You mean so much to everyone around you”
* Similar generic greeting card language

Create guardrails that discourage generic filler.

5. Opening Lines

Strengthen openings.

Cards should preferably begin with:

* a memory
* an observation
* a callback
* a recent event

Avoid leading with generic occasion statements whenever possible.

6. Ending Lines

Strengthen endings.

Avoid:

* “Have a great day”
* “Wishing you the best”
* Generic closings

Prefer endings that reference the relationship, memory, joke, goal, or future event.

7. Card Review Audit

Generate sample cards using:

* spouse
* parent
* friend
* child

For each sample:

* identify strengths
* identify weaknesses
* explain what changed

8. No New Features

Do not build:

* new dashboard components
* new profile sections
* new memory systems
* new scores
* new databases

This sprint is focused entirely on card quality.

Deliverables

1. Current card quality assessment
2. Proposed prompt improvements
3. Sample before and after cards
4. Expected impact
5. Implementation plan

Wait for approval before changing production prompts.
