Creative Direction Reset: We Are Designing Greeting Cards, Not Artwork

After reviewing the generated humor cards and comparing them to successful retail humor cards, we've identified the core issue.

Current direction:
Premium artwork with a humorous theme.

Desired direction:
A greeting card that creates an immediate smile, laugh, or emotional reaction within 1 to 2 seconds.

Important realization:

Customers do not buy greeting cards because the artwork is beautiful.

Customers buy greeting cards because the idea is funny.

The artwork supports the joke.

The joke is the product.

New creative standard:

Every humor card must contain BOTH:

1. A clear visual joke
2. A short front cover caption

The image and caption should work together.

The customer should immediately understand the joke from several feet away.

Avoid:

• Fine art that requires interpretation
• Clever concepts without a punchline
• Beautiful images that are only mildly amusing
• Cards that feel like artwork first and greeting cards second

Examples:

Lord Fluffington

Image:
Cat knocking a royal goblet off a banquet table while nobles panic.

Front:
"I REGRET NOTHING."

Coffee Before Humanity

Image:
A giant coffee mug being worshipped like a sacred artifact.

Front:
"COFFEE. THEN WE CAN DISCUSS YOUR PROBLEMS."

Golf Goose

Image:
Goose aggressively defending a nest full of golf balls.

Front:
"I'VE INVESTED TOO MUCH TO WALK AWAY NOW."

Wine Dachshund

Image:
Tiny dachshund judging wine with absurd seriousness.

Front:
"THIS PAIRS WELL WITH POOR DECISIONS."

BBQ Bulldog

Image:
Bulldog judging a barbecue competition.

Front:
"EVERYONE IS WRONG EXCEPT ME."

Creative rules:

• Humor should land immediately.
• Captions should be short.
• Captions should be highly relatable.
• Avoid generic birthday wording.
• Avoid Hallmark language.
• Avoid inspirational language.
• Avoid cute for the sake of cute.
• Prioritize laugh, smirk, or "that's so true."

Before generating additional cards:

Create 15 humor concepts.

For each concept provide:

• Card title
• Artwork description
• Front cover caption
• Why the joke works

Do NOT generate artwork yet.

First prove the concepts are funny.

Once concepts are approved, generate artwork for the strongest concepts only.

The benchmark is not premium art.

The benchmark is whether someone browsing a card rack would immediately pick it up and show it to a friend.
