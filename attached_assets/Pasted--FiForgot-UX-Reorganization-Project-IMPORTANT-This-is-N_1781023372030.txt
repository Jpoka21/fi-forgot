# FiForgot UX Reorganization Project

## IMPORTANT

This is NOT a new feature project.

This is NOT a database redesign.

This is NOT a backend refactor.

Keep existing functionality.

Keep existing APIs.

Keep existing data structures.

Keep existing card generation logic.

Keep existing profile questions.

Keep existing updates.

Keep existing health scoring.

Keep existing Brownie Points.

Keep existing recipient records.

The goal is to reorganize the experience so it feels dramatically simpler.

---

# Product Goal

A first time user should be able to:

Sign up

Add a person

Generate a card

In under 2 minutes.

Desired user reaction:

"That was easy."

---

# Core Philosophy

Current experience feels like:

Recipient → Configuration → Questions → Briefing → Card

New experience should feel like:

Person → Occasion → Card

The user should feel like they are managing people they care about.

Not filling out forms.

Not maintaining database records.

---

# NEW DASHBOARD STRUCTURE

The dashboard should be reorganized into three primary sections.

## SECTION 1: UPCOMING MOMENTS

This becomes the hero section.

This is the reason people pay for FiForgot.

Show:

Steve
Birthday in 12 days
Generate Card

Mom
Birthday in 21 days
Generate Card

Dad
Father's Day in 5 days
Generate Card

Wife
Anniversary in 30 days
Generate Card

The dashboard should immediately answer:

"What needs my attention?"

---

## SECTION 2: QUICK CARD

Add a prominent new feature.

⚡ Send a Quick Card

Purpose:

Allow users to generate a one off card without creating a recipient profile.

Flow:

Who is the card for?

What is the occasion?

Anything you'd like mentioned?

Generate card.

Target completion time:

Under 60 seconds.

After generation show:

"Want us to remember Steve so future cards are even better?"

Button:

Add Steve to FiForgot

This turns Quick Cards into a recipient acquisition funnel.

---

## SECTION 3: YOUR PEOPLE

Display recipient cards.

Examples:

Steve

Mom

Dad

Wife

Alex

Athena

Each card shows:

Relationship

Next upcoming occasion

Simple status

No clutter.

No large profile completion indicators.

No giant health score displays.

# MOVE SETTINGS OUT OF RECIPIENT PROFILES

Create a dedicated Settings area.

Move:

Mailing address

Card delivery

Approval timing

Autopilot settings

Email timing

Workflow settings

Shipping preferences

Address management

Out of the main recipient profile.

These are FiForgot settings.

They are not part of the recipient.

---

# HEALTH SCORE CHANGES

Keep health scoring.

Do not remove it.

However:

Move health scoring into a supporting role.

It should power:

Relationship insights

Suggested questions

Recommended updates

Recipient prioritization

Do not make it the centerpiece of onboarding.

Do not make it the centerpiece of recipient profiles.

Health scoring should work behind the scenes.

---

# RECIPIENT VISUAL IDENTITY

Do NOT use photos.

Do NOT use AI generated faces.

Do NOT require uploads.

Replace current initials based avatars with relationship based icons inside branded circles.

Examples:

❤️ Spouse

👨 Dad

👩 Mom

👦 Son

👧 Daughter

🤝 Friend

🏢 Client

🐶 Pet

These should be visually appealing and consistent with FiForgot branding.

Users should recognize relationships at a glance.

---

# MEMORY VAULT FEEL

Recipient profiles should feel warmer and more personal.

Use concepts from the Memory Vault design direction.

Focus on:

Memories

Updates

Stories

Inside jokes

Things we know

The profile should feel like:

"This is Steve."

Not:

"This is Recipient #42."

---

# WHAT NOT TO DO

Do not remove existing functionality.

Do not remove profile questions.

Do not remove updates.

Do not remove health scoring.

Do not remove Brownie Points.

Do not remove autopilot.

Do not redesign database structures.

Do not create additional onboarding friction.

Do not require profile completion.

Do not block card generation.

---

# SUCCESS METRICS

Primary:

Time to first card

Target:
Under 2 minutes

Secondary:

More Quick Card usage

More recipient creation from Quick Cards

Higher card approval rates

More repeat usage

---

# FINAL DESIGN TEST

A completely new user should be able to log in and instantly understand:

1. What needs attention
2. How to send a card
3. Who is important to them

Without explanation.

If the design requires explanation, simplify further.
