Update the homepage and routing so the site supports two separate experiences: Personal and Business.

We are not creating two separate products. This is one website, one brand, and one card sending engine, but with two different user experiences after the visitor chooses their path.

Keep the Dave in the Doghouse brand, humor, colors, and emotional style. Do not make the business side feel corporate or like CRM software.

IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES

Do not remove the current personal version.
Do not make the homepage cluttered.
Do not overuse the word AI.
Use “real greeting cards,” “thoughtful cards,” and “relationship maintenance.”
Avoid corporate buzzwords like enterprise, pipeline, workflow, or CRM.
Keep Dave in the Doghouse as the brand mascot.
The business side should feel like Dave grew up a little, not like a different company.
Make all mobile sections stacked, clean, and easy to tap.
