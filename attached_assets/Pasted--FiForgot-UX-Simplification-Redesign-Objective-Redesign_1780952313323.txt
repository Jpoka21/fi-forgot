# FiForgot UX Simplification Redesign

## Objective

Redesign FiForgot around a single principle:

**"That was easy."**

A first time user should be able to sign up, add a recipient, and generate their first card in under 2 minutes.

The goal is NOT to remove functionality.

The goal is to hide complexity and make FiForgot feel effortless.

---

# Core Product Philosophy

Current experience feels like:

Recipient → Configuration → Questions → Briefing → Card

New experience should feel like:

Person → Occasion → Card

The user should never feel like they are managing a CRM.

They should feel like they are building a collection of people they care about.

---

# Rule #1

Never block card generation.

If the user only knows:

Name: Steve

Relationship: Friend

Birthday: June 20

One memory: Loves camping

FiForgot should generate the card.

After generation, FiForgot may suggest additional information that would improve future cards.

Do not require profile completion.

Do not require additional questions.

Do not force onboarding steps.

---

# Rule #2

Recipients are the center of the product.

Everything should be organized around people.

Not settings.

Not workflows.

Not health scores.

Not database records.

When a user clicks a recipient, they should immediately see information about that person.

---

# Remove "Edit Recipient"

The phrase "Edit Recipient" should be eliminated from the product.

Users should feel like they are viewing a person, not editing a database record.

Replace with a recipient profile experience.

Example:

# Steve

Friend

Birthday in 42 days

We know 5 things about Steve

Last update 2 months ago

## Help Us Write Better Cards

Display only ONE question at a time.

Example:

"What is something Steve has been excited about recently?"

Answer

Next Question

Never show a large wall of profile questions.

---

# Move Settings Out Of Recipient Profile

Create a separate Settings area.

Move the following out of the main recipient page:

Mailing Address

Card Delivery

Approval Timing

Autopilot Settings

Email Timing

Workflow Preferences

Card Handling Preferences

These are FiForgot settings.

They are not part of the recipient profile.

---

# Health Score Changes

Do NOT remove health scoring.

Health scoring remains valuable.

However:

Health scoring should become a background intelligence system.

Do not make it a primary first time user feature.

Do not place it at the center of recipient onboarding.

Health score should help FiForgot determine:

Who needs updates

Who needs follow up questions

Which relationships are becoming stale

Where card quality may decline

It should support the experience.

It should not define the experience.

---

# Card Generation Philosophy

Card generation should always succeed.

If data quality is low:

Generate the card.

Then display:

"This card was generated with limited information. Add a few more details about Steve to make future cards even more personal."

Never stop the user.

Never create friction.

---

# Success Metric

Do NOT optimize for:

Profile completion

Health score completion

Questions answered

Timeline activity

Optimize for:

Time to first card

Target:

Under 2 minutes

The first card is the product.

Everything else exists to improve future cards.

---

# Final Principle

FiForgot should feel like a collection of people.

Not a collection of forms.

When a user clicks Steve, they should feel like they are looking at Steve.

Not configuring Steve.
