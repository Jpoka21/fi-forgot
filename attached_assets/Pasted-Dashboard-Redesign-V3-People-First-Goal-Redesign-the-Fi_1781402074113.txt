Dashboard Redesign V3 — People First

Goal

Redesign the FiForgot dashboard around people and actions rather than widgets, metrics, scores, and cards.

Core Principle

The dashboard should feel like a list of people the user cares about, not a CRM.

A user should understand what needs attention within 5 seconds.

Design Direction

Think Apple Contacts.

Think Messages.

Think relationship management.

Do NOT think HubSpot, Salesforce, or a widget dashboard.

Avoid large cards, oversized sections, excessive white space, health rings, profile percentages, and dashboard metrics.

Dashboard Layout

Section 1 — We Got Your Back

Very compact.

Maximum 3 items.

Priority order:

1. Upcoming events
2. Drafts requiring review
3. Relationships needing updates

Examples:

• Mom’s birthday is in 12 days
• Sarah’s anniversary card is ready
• Dad has not been updated in 4 months

No large cards.

No scrolling feed.

Just concise action items.

Section 2 — Quick Card

Compact panel near the top.

Title:

Need a Card Right Now?

Description:

Create a personalized card for anyone, even if they are not one of Your Important People.

Primary CTA:

Create Quick Card

Supporting text:

No recipient setup required.

Quick Card Flow

Collect:

• Recipient name
• Relationship
• Occasion

Generate card immediately.

Do NOT automatically create a recipient.

After card generation show:

Make future cards even better.

Add [Recipient Name] to Your Important People and we’ll remember important dates, memories, and updates so every future card becomes more personal.

Buttons:

[Add To My People]
[Not Now]

If Add To My People is selected:

• Create recipient automatically
• Prefill name
• Prefill relationship
• Open recipient profile
• Prompt:

“Tell us something new about [Recipient Name].”

No additional setup forms.

Section 3 — Your Important People

Add search.

Add recipient button.

Group people into:

Needs Attention

Healthy Relationships

Each row should be compact.

Each row should contain:

• Name
• Upcoming event if applicable
• Most recent meaningful memory/update
• Recommended next action

Examples:

Mom

Birthday in 12 days

“She loved Alex’s school play.”

Recommended Action:
Create Birthday Card

⸻

Dad

No updates in 4 months

“Bought a fishing boat.”

Recommended Action:
Tell Us Something New

⸻

Sarah

Anniversary card ready

Recommended Action:
Review Draft

Row Actions

Do NOT place 4 to 6 buttons on every row.

Show one primary recommended action.

Allow additional actions from an overflow menu or when opening the profile.

Events

Do NOT place a large Add Event button on every row.

Events should primarily be managed from the recipient profile.

Additionally, the Tell Us Something New workflow should support:

• General update
• Important event or milestone

Examples:

• Retirement
• New baby
• New job
• Graduation
• Wedding
• New home
• Custom milestone

If an important milestone is entered, save it both as:

• A relationship update
• A future event where appropriate

Mobile Requirements

Mobile is a first-class experience.

Rows should remain compact.

Show:

• Name
• One line of context
• One recommended action

Hide secondary actions behind a menu.

Avoid horizontal scrolling.

Success Criteria

The dashboard should feel significantly simpler than the current version.

The focus should be:

People first.

Memories second.

Cards third.

Everything else should fade into the background.
