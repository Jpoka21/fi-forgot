PRIMARY GOAL

FiForgot is not just a card generator.

FiForgot is building a relationship memory system that becomes more intelligent over time.

The system should collect useful relationship information while keeping the user experience fast and simple.

The user should never feel like they are filling out a CRM.

The system should quietly build a relationship database in the background.

## RECIPIENT DATABASE ARCHITECTURE

Every recipient must have:

recipient_id (unique)
user_id (owner of recipient)
first_name
relationship_type

All recipient data must always be scoped to the logged in user.

Never identify recipients by name alone.

Never share recipient data across users.

If a user creates a recipient whose first name already exists in their account:

Display:

"We found another recipient named Joe."

Options:

Use Existing Joe
Create New Joe

Do not automatically merge recipients.

Do not require last names.

Do not require email addresses.

Do not require phone numbers.

Keep recipient creation friction extremely low.

## AI WRITING RULES

Do not automatically write sweet cards.

Do not assume heartfelt is better.

Write what this sender would realistically say.

Use relationship type heavily.

Use communication style heavily.

Use emotional openness heavily.

Use specific details whenever available.

Use memories before compliments.

Use stories before generic praise.

Do not overuse:

You mean the world to me

Life would not be the same without you

Rare person

One of a kind

Impossible not to like

Or similar generic greeting card language

For funny male friendships:

Favor teasing

Inside jokes

Shared stupidity

Understated appreciation

For siblings:

Mix annoyance and loyalty

For parents:

Favor gratitude

Life lessons

Sacrifice

Pride

For spouses:

Favor real life details over generic romance

For professional relationships:

Remain professional and appropriate
