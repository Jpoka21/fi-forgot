# FiForgot Dashboard Simplification & Visual Hierarchy Pass

IMPORTANT

Do NOT redesign the dashboard from scratch.

Do NOT remove functionality.

Do NOT change database structures.

Do NOT change backend logic.

Do NOT remove Brownie Points.

Do NOT remove Relationship Health.

Do NOT remove Insights.

This is a visual hierarchy and UX refinement project.

The goal is to make the dashboard feel cleaner, calmer, more premium, and easier to understand.

---

# PRIMARY GOAL

When a user lands on the dashboard, their eyes should naturally follow this order:

1. Upcoming Moments
2. Your People
3. Everything Else

If all sections feel equally important, the design has failed.

---

# KEEP

Keep:

We Got Your Back

Upcoming Moments

Your People

Relationship Insights

Relationship Health

Quick Card

Brownie Points

All existing functionality

---

# VISUAL HIERARCHY

The dashboard currently feels crowded because every section has equal visual weight.

Create stronger hierarchy.

Make some things visually dominant.

Make some things visually quiet.

Not everything should compete for attention.

---

# SECTION 1: UPCOMING MOMENTS

This should be the hero section.

It is the core reason people use FiForgot.

Increase visual prominence.

Increase spacing.

Make cards easier to scan.

Reduce visual clutter.

Focus on:

Name

Occasion

Days remaining

Generate Card button

Everything else should be secondary.

---

# SECTION 2: YOUR PEOPLE

Move this higher visually.

Increase card size.

Make recipients feel like people.

Not database records.

Use relationship icons instead of initials.

Examples:

❤️ Wife

👨 Dad

👩 Mom

🤝 Friend

👦 Son

👧 Daughter

Each person card should show:

Name

Relationship

Next occasion

Nothing more.

Keep it simple.

---

# SECTION 3: QUICK CARD

Move Quick Card much higher.

Place it directly below Your People or directly below Upcoming Moments.

Quick Card should feel like:

Need a card right now?

Generate one in under 60 seconds.

Do not fully build the workflow yet.

Focus on placement and presentation.

---

# RELATIONSHIP INSIGHTS

Demote visually.

Keep the functionality.

Reduce visual weight.

This is supporting information.

Not primary information.

---

# RELATIONSHIP HEALTH

Keep it.

Do not remove it.

Move lower on the page.

Reduce visual emphasis.

Use it as supporting intelligence.

Not a hero section.

---

# BROWNIE POINTS

Keep Brownie Points.

Make them feel fun.

Not like a KPI dashboard.

Not like business software.

Think reward.

Think encouragement.

Think progress.

---

# WHITESPACE

Increase whitespace significantly.

The current dashboard feels crowded.

Add more breathing room between sections.

Add more breathing room inside cards.

Allow important sections to breathe.

---

# BORDERS

Reduce heavy borders.

Many cards currently compete visually.

Use fewer borders.

Use softer dividers.

Allow buttons and actions to stand out instead.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Strengthen hierarchy.

Large headlines.

Medium section titles.

Small supporting text.

A user should instantly know:

What is most important

What is secondary

What is optional

---

# PREMIUM FEEL

Use the design concepts already created as inspiration.

The dashboard should feel:

Cleaner

More confident

More premium

More modern

Less busy

Less crowded

Less like a collection of widgets

More like a cohesive product

---

# FINAL TEST

A new user should be able to understand the dashboard in under 10 seconds.

They should immediately know:

Who needs attention

How to send a card

Who matters to them

Without explanation.

After implementation, provide screenshots before making any additional dashboard changes.
