# FiForgot Phase 8: Card Generation Quality Sprint

Objective:

Pause new feature development.

Focus entirely on improving card quality so generated cards feel personal, human, and difficult to distinguish from something the sender would have written themselves.

The goal is not more cards.

The goal is better cards.

---

## Core Principle

FiForgot is not an AI card writer.

FiForgot is a relationship memory system that helps users express thoughts they already feel.

Every generated card should feel:

"I would have written this if I had more time."

Not:

"AI wrote this for me."

---

## New Card Quality Requirements

Every generated card should be evaluated against these standards.

### 1. Specificity

Cards must use recipient specific information naturally.

Bad:

"Hope you have a wonderful birthday."

Good:

"It's been fun hearing about your pickleball obsession this year. I still can't decide whether you're becoming an athlete or just collecting hobbies."

If a card could be sent to 100 people without modification, it fails.

---

### 2. Memory Integration

Use multiple memory sources together.

Available memory sources:

Profile Questions

Fresh Updates

Follow Up Answers

Event Briefings

Previous Card History

Timeline Memories

Do not simply mention one memory.

Weave memories together naturally.

Example:

Instead of:

"Congrats on finishing your kitchen."

Prefer:

"Between the kitchen renovation finally wrapping up and your new pickleball phase, it feels like you've somehow turned this year into two completely different adventures."

---

### 3. Relationship Specific Voice

Create different writing styles based on relationship type.

Examples:

Spouse:
More intimate, appreciative, emotionally warm.

Parent:
Grateful, reflective, family focused.

Child:
Encouraging, proud, playful.

Friend:
Casual, conversational, humorous.

Coworker:
Professional but personal.

Grandparent:
Warm, respectful, memory driven.

The generated voice should change significantly depending on relationship.

---

### 4. Stronger Openings

Avoid generic openings.

Avoid:

"Happy Birthday."

"Just wanted to wish you..."

"Hope you have a great day."

Instead open with:

A memory

An observation

A joke

A recent life update

A meaningful reflection

The first sentence should immediately feel personal.

---

### 5. Stronger Closings

Avoid:

"Have a great day."

"Wishing you all the best."

End with:

Appreciation

A shared memory

Optimism about something happening in their life

A relationship specific sentiment

The final paragraph should feel memorable.

---

### 6. Storytelling

When enough information exists, reference short stories instead of facts.

Bad:

"You enjoy pickleball."

Better:

"It still makes me laugh that a casual attempt to try pickleball somehow turned into a regular part of your week."

Cards should feel like they know the person.

---

### 7. Avoid AI Language

Create a banned phrase list.

Examples:

Wishing you all the best

On this special day

May your year be filled with

Celebrate you

Cherish every moment

Another trip around the sun

Warmest wishes

Heartfelt wishes

Wishing you happiness and success

Avoid repetitive AI card language.

---

### 8. Personal Detail Priority

When card context exceeds model limits, prioritize information in this order:

1. Event Briefing Answers
2. Fresh Updates less than 90 days old
3. Follow Up Answers
4. Profile Question Answers
5. Fresh Updates 90 to 180 days old
6. Older Timeline Context
7. Previous Cards

Most recent information should have the strongest influence.

---

### 9. Memory Density Target

Every card should contain:

At least 2 specific personal references.

At least 1 recent memory when available.

At least 1 relationship specific observation.

Cards should never feel generic when memory exists.

---

### 10. Internal Quality Score

Create an internal quality scoring function.

Score:

Specificity

Memory Usage

Relationship Voice

Opening Quality

Closing Quality

AI Phrase Detection

Return score 0 to 100.

Store score with generated draft.

This score is internal only and not shown to users.

---

## Testing Requirement

Generate and review sample cards for:

Spouse

Parent

Child

Friend

Coworker

Grandparent

Evaluate failures.

Refine prompts until cards consistently feel personal, natural, and emotionally authentic.

Success metric:

Users should feel:

"That sounds like something I would actually send."

Not:

"That sounds like AI."
