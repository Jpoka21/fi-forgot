Implement Brownie Points Foundation.

GOAL

Brownie Points should become the positive reinforcement engine behind FiForgot.

The purpose is not gamification.

The purpose is to reward thoughtful relationship habits.

Users should feel:

"I'm doing a better job keeping up with the people I care about."

NOT:

"I'm collecting meaningless points."

This is Version 1.

Keep it simple.

---

BROWNIE POINTS MODEL

Create:

browniePointsBalance

at the account level.

Store lifetime total:

lifetimeBrowniePoints

Store transaction history:

BrowniePointTransaction

Fields:

id
userId
recipientId (nullable)
actionType
points
description
createdAt

Never overwrite.

Always create transactions.

---

+25 bonus points

+5 Brownie Points

Approve and send card:
+25 Brownie Points

Send card at least 7 days before event:
+25 bonus points

Add new recipient:
+15 Brownie Points

Add birthday:
+10 Brownie Points

Add anniversary:
+10 Brownie Points

---

ANTI-SPAM RULES

Prevent abuse.

Fresh Updates:

Maximum:
3 rewarded updates per recipient per day

Card generation:

Maximum:
5 rewarded generations per day

All other actions:

Award once per legitimate action.

---

RELATIONSHIP REINFORCEMENT

Every point earning action should reinforce the mission.

Good examples:

You helped future cards become more personal.

You stayed ahead of an important date.

You invested in a relationship.

Bad examples:

Level Up!

Combo Bonus!

XP Earned!

Avoid game language.

---

FUTURE PREPARATION

Store all transactions permanently.

Future versions may use points for:

Premium card designs

Free card credits

Seasonal rewards

Priority features

Do not build redemption yet.

Only build the foundation.

---

SUCCESS CRITERIA

Users begin noticing Brownie Points naturally.

Points reward the exact behaviors that improve FiForgot.

The system feels encouraging rather than gamified.

Brownie Points become a visible representation of relationship effort and consistency.
