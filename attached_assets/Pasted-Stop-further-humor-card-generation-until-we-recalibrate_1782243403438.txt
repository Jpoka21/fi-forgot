Stop further humor card generation until we recalibrate the creative direction.

The artwork quality is strong. The problem is not execution quality, it is humor execution.

Current outputs are producing:

Premium Art + Theme = Interesting

We need:

Premium Art + Absurd Situation = Funny

New creative rule for all cards in the Humor category:

A viewer should understand the joke within 2 seconds without reading a caption.

Every humor card must contain at least one of the following:

• Visual punchline
• Absurd situation
• Unexpected contradiction
• Human behavior performed seriously by an animal
• Overly dramatic treatment of something mundane

Do NOT create simple portraits, still lifes, or themed artwork.

Do NOT create:
• Animal wearing clothes and staring at camera
• Object with a face sitting in a chair
• Coffee cup under dramatic lighting
• Generic aristocratic portraits

These are premium illustrations but not comedy.

Target examples:

Lord Fluffington
Not:
Cat in royal clothing

Instead:
Cat being worshipped by servants
Cat sitting on a throne made of tuna cans
Cat knocking priceless objects off a royal table while remaining dignified

Not:

Creative standard:

The image should feel like a museum quality oil painting of something completely ridiculous.

The viewer should smile or laugh before reading the title.

Before generating the remaining batch:

Humor is now the primary success metric. Premium artistic quality remains required, but visual comedy takes priority over elegance.
