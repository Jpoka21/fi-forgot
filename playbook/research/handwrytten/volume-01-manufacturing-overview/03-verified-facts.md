## Governing Principles

- Only audited and source-captured facts may enter this document.
- Official vendor statements remain vendor facts, not F.I. Forgot standards.
- Developer documentation establishes supported capability, not universal operational use.
- Support documentation establishes documented workflow, not undisclosed manufacturing detail.
- Marketing claims are excluded unless the fact concerns the existence of the marketing statement itself.
- Historical facts must be labeled historical.
- Qualifications are part of the fact and must not be removed downstream.
- A fact may be revised only through a documented evidence audit update.
- Missing fact IDs must not be reused.
- HOLD and REJECT candidates remain outside the verified baseline.

The following classifications are used in this volume:


| Classification              | Meaning                                                                                                                                       |

| **Support Documentation**   | A fact supported by an official Handwrytten support guide that documents a customer-facing workflow or operational boundary                   |
| **Developer Documentation** | A fact supported by official Handwrytten API documentation that establishes platform capability or endpoint structure                         |
