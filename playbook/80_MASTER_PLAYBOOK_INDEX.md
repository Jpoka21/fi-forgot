# Master Playbook Index

> **Purpose:**  

> This document is the master table of contents and navigation guide for the entire F.I. Forgot Playbook. It explains how the playbook is organized, how contributors should use it, and where to find guidance for every aspect of the product. Every designer, engineer, product manager, QA engineer, and AI coding assistant should begin here after reading **71_EXECUTIVE_[SUMMARY.md](http://SUMMARY.md)**.

---

# About This Playbook

The F.I. Forgot Playbook is the complete operating manual for designing, building, testing, maintaining, and evolving the product.

It captures years of product thinking in a single, unified source of truth.

Every document exists to answer one question:

**What would a world class Relationship Concierge do here?**

The answer to that question should guide every decision.

# Product Development Workflow

Every implementation should follow this sequence.

Understand the philosophy.

↓

Review the relevant playbook documents.

↓

Identify reusable components.

↓

Design if necessary.

↓

Implement.

↓

Test.

↓

Review accessibility.

↓

Review responsiveness.

↓

Review performance.

↓

QA approval.

↓

Launch.

↓

Measure.

↓

Improve.

No step should be skipped.

When documentation appears to conflict, use the following priority.

1. Executive Summary

2. Product Philosophy

3. Relationship Concierge Philosophy

4. Trust and Privacy

5. Accessibility

6. Design System

7. Frontend Architecture

8. Component Library

9. Screen Specifications

10. Implementation Rules

11. Development Guides

If uncertainty remains, update the documentation before updating the code.

# Core Principles

Everything in the playbook is built around these principles.

Relationships come first.

People come before technology.

Thoughtfulness comes before automation.

Trust comes before growth.

Consistency builds confidence.

Accessibility is mandatory.

Performance shapes perception.

AI supports people.

The design system is the source of visual truth.

The playbook is the source of product truth.

---

# What Must Never Change

No matter how the product evolves, these principles remain constant.

F.I. Forgot is a Relationship Concierge.

Relationships are the center of the experience.

Greeting cards are the first service, not the entire product.

Users remain in control.

AI remains an assistant.

Privacy is non negotiable.

Accessibility is a requirement.

Warmth is part of the brand.

Thoughtfulness is the product.

Everything else may evolve.

These principles should not.

---

# Maintaining the Playbook

The playbook is a living document.

When new features are introduced:

Update the relevant philosophy.

Update the design system if needed.

Update implementation guidance.

Update testing documentation.

Update launch requirements.

Update this index.

Documentation should evolve alongside the product.

The playbook should never become outdated.

---

# Success Criteria

The playbook succeeds when:

New contributors become productive quickly.

Design decisions remain consistent.

Development accelerates.

Quality improves.

Accessibility remains excellent.

The product evolves without losing its identity.

Every contributor understands not only **what** to build, but **why** it exists.

# Final Manifesto

This playbook is more than documentation.

It is the written expression of the values behind F.I. Forgot.

It reminds us that software is not the goal.

Relationships are.

Every screen.

Every interaction.

Every animation.

Every sentence.

Every line of code.

Every product decision.

Should help someone become a little more thoughtful toward another human being.

If we remain faithful to that purpose, F.I. Forgot will never simply be another application.

It will become something far more meaningful.

It will become a trusted Relationship Concierge that quietly helps people show up for the ones who matter most.

That is the purpose of this playbook.

That is the standard.

That is F.I. Forgot.
