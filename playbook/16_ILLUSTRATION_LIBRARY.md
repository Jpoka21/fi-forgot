# F.I. Forgot 2.0 — Illustration Library

## Purpose

This document is the master source of truth for every illustration used in the F.I. Forgot consumer experience.

Every illustration should have a clear purpose, consistent style, approved filename, and production status.

No illustration should be created without first being added to this library.

No placeholder should remain in the application once its production illustration is complete.

---

# Illustration Style

All illustrations must follow the approved F.I. Forgot Character Bible and Illustration Style Guide.

Characteristics:

- Warm
- Premium
- Emotional
- Soft painterly style
- Rounded forms
- Muted colors
- Cream backgrounds
- Gentle afternoon lighting
- No embedded marketing text
- No UI inside illustrations
- Relationship first
- Never SaaS
- Never CRM

---

# Production Workflow

For every illustration:

1. Design concept
2. Generate draft
3. Refine until approved
4. Export production master
5. Export optimized WebP
6. Save with final filename
7. Replace placeholder in application
8. Mark complete below

# Asset Specifications

## 001 — Homepage Hero Dave

**Status:** ✅ Approved

**Filename**

homepage_hero_dave.webp

**Purpose**

Introduce Doghouse Dave and the emotional problem that F.I. Forgot solves.

**Placement**

Homepage hero.

**Dimensions**

3000 × 2250 master

**Background**

Transparent.

**Composition**

Dave sits comfortably inside the doghouse with the door open, wearing his approved outfit and expression. The doghouse feels cozy rather than punishing. Warm afternoon lighting, soft landscaping, and plenty of negative space allow the homepage copy to breathe.
