# F.I. Forgot Copy Guide

---

# Purpose

This document defines how F.I. Forgot communicates throughout the entire product.

Every word contributes to the experience.

Users are not interacting with software.

They are interacting with a thoughtful Relationship Concierge that quietly helps them become better husbands, wives, sons, daughters, parents, friends, coworkers, managers, business owners, and human beings.

The product should feel:

* Warm

* Calm

* Helpful

* Intelligent

* Trustworthy

* Premium

* Personal

Never robotic.

Never corporate.

Never overly emotional.

Never overly clever.

The product should always feel like a thoughtful concierge quietly working in the background.

---

# The Core Voice

The product voice can be summarized in six words:

**Warm. Calm. Personal. Confident. Helpful. Human.**

Every screen should feel as though a concierge is speaking directly to one person.

Not to thousands of customers.

---

# Personality Principles

The concierge is:

* thoughtful

* observant

* encouraging

* organized

* respectful

* emotionally intelligent

* quietly confident

The concierge is never:

* loud

* salesy

* pushy

* sarcastic

* robotic

* cheesy

* dramatic

* childish

---

# Relationship First

The product never focuses on greeting cards.

It focuses on people.

Instead of saying:

> Create a Birthday Card

Say:

> Celebrate Sarah's birthday.

Instead of:

> Recipient

Say:

> Sarah

Whenever possible, use names.

Relationships always come before features.

---

# Natural Language

The product should sound like normal conversation.

Instead of:

> Your operation completed successfully.

Say:

> Everything is ready.

Instead of:

> Synchronization complete.

Say:

> You're all caught up.

---

# Short Is Better

Every sentence should be as short as possible without losing warmth.

Avoid paragraphs.

Avoid explanations unless they are necessary.

Users scan.

They do not read manuals.

---

# Confidence

The concierge should never sound uncertain.

Avoid phrases like:

* Maybe

* Hopefully

* We think

* We believe

* It appears

Instead say:

* You're ready.

* We've taken care of it.

* Everything looks good.

---

# Friendly Without Being Casual

Avoid slang.

Avoid internet culture.

Avoid excessive enthusiasm.

Good:

> Nice work.

Better:

> That's one more thoughtful moment taken care of.

Bad:

> Awesome!!

Bad:

> Sweet!

Bad:

> You're crushing it!

---

# Positive Language

Focus on what users gain.

Instead of:

> Don't forget birthdays.

Say:

> Stay connected with the people who matter most.

---

Headlines should be:

* short

* calm

* clear

* emotionally meaningful

Keep the little moments alive

Avoid headlines like:

Dashboard

Management Portal

Recipient Information

Card Generator

User Profile

Settings Menu

Examples:

The more we learn, the more meaningful every card becomes.

# Error Messages

Never blame users.

Never sound technical.

Good:

Something interrupted that.

Please try again.

---

Good:

We couldn't save that just yet.

Nothing has been lost.

Please try again.

---

Good:

We couldn't reach our servers.

We'll reconnect as soon as possible.

---

Avoid:

Fatal Error

Unexpected Exception

Unknown Error

Invalid Request

Stack Trace

---

# Success Messages

Celebrate progress.

Examples:

Saved.

Memory added.

Your relationship just became a little stronger.

Everything is ready.

Card draft updated.

You're all set.

Nice work.

---

# Loading Messages

Loading copy should reassure users.

Examples:

Getting everything ready...

Building your draft...

Finding the right memories...

Organizing your relationships...

Almost there...

Preparing something thoughtful...

Looking through your shared moments...

---

Never use fake progress.

Never exaggerate AI abilities.

---

# Notifications

Notifications should feel helpful.

Not urgent.

Examples:

Sarah's birthday is coming up in two weeks.

You have time to make it personal.

---

We learned something new about Michael.

Would you like to add it to his profile?

---

A handwritten card is on its way.

We'll let you know when it's delivered.

---

Avoid:

ALERT

WARNING

URGENT

ACTION REQUIRED

---

# Concierge Prompts

Prompts should invite conversation.

Not interrogation.

Good:

Tell me about Sarah.

What's something she always laughs about?

What's one memory you'll never forget?

What has she been excited about lately?

Has anything changed recently?

What's something only the two of you would understand?

What would make this card feel unmistakably personal?

---

Avoid:

Enter recipient data.

Provide personality attributes.

Fill out profile.

---

# AI Transparency

The concierge should be honest.

Never pretend AI knows something it doesn't.

Good:

Here's a first draft based on everything you've shared.

---

Good:

I used your memories and previous cards to make this more personal.

---

Good:

If something doesn't sound like you, change it.

---

Never say:

I remember everything.

I know how she feels.

I know what you're thinking.

I understand your relationship.

---

# Privacy And Trust Copy

Privacy language should be calm.

Examples:

Your memories belong to you.

We use what you share to make future cards more personal.

You're always in control.

You can edit or delete anything.

We never sell your personal memories.

Your relationships stay private.

---

# Billing Copy

Billing should feel respectful.

Examples:

Choose the plan that's right for you.

No surprises.

Cancel whenever you like.

Your subscription keeps your Relationship Concierge working year round.

You're in control of your membership.

---

Avoid pressure.

Avoid countdown timers.

Avoid fear based messaging.

---

# Accessibility Friendly Copy

Use plain language.

Avoid idioms.

Avoid complicated wording.

Avoid jargon.

Keep reading levels approachable.

Labels should describe actions.

Examples:

Save relationship

Open timeline

Add memory

Review draft

Choose card

Never rely on color alone.

Always pair color with text.

# Writing Rules

Always write in active voice.

Prefer contractions.

Examples:

You're

We'll

It's

Don't

Avoid passive voice.

Avoid unnecessary adjectives.

Avoid filler.

Every sentence should earn its place.

# The Copy Test

Before any piece of copy ships, ask:

Does this sound like software?

If yes, rewrite it.

Does this sound like marketing?

If yes, rewrite it.

Does this sound like AI?

If yes, rewrite it.

Does this sound like a thoughtful Relationship Concierge who genuinely wants to help someone become more thoughtful?

If yes, it belongs in F.I. Forgot.

---

# Final Principle

Every sentence should strengthen trust.

Every word should strengthen relationships.

Every interaction should leave the user feeling a little more thoughtful than they were a moment ago.

That is the voice of F.I. Forgot.
