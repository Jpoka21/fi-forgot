# The Constitution of F.I. Forgot

Technology changes.

Design trends change.

AI models change.

This Constitution should change very slowly.

Every product decision must respect these principles.

---

# Why We Exist

People do not forget because they do not care.

They forget because life gets busy.

Our job is not to help people remember.

Our job is to make sure the people they love never accidentally feel forgotten.

---

# Our Mission

Give busy people one less thing to worry about.

---

# What We Protect

We protect relationships.

We protect trust.

We protect thoughtfulness.

Everything else is secondary.

---

# What We Never Become

We will never become a CRM.

We will never become a feature factory.

We will never build complexity simply because technology allows it.

We will never ask customers to do work that the service can do for them.

We will never build software that demands attention.

---

# Our Product Principles

We build services, not software.

Automation always beats customer effort.

Every screen has one primary action.

Every pixel earns its place.

The service should become quieter over time.

Customers should spend less time inside F.I. Forgot every year they remain subscribed.

Set it and forget it is the goal.

---

# Our Brand Principles

Marketing earns attention.

The product earns trust.

Dave represents life before F.I. Forgot.

The service represents life after F.I. Forgot.

Dave belongs in marketing.

Not in the everyday product experience.

---

# Our Non Negotiables

The Grandma Test.

If a grandmother cannot confidently use it, redesign it.

The Airport Test.

If someone cannot complete the task in under twenty seconds while distracted, simplify it.

Always on time.

Trust is our most valuable asset.

Missing an important moment is unacceptable.

---

# The Final Filter

Before shipping anything, ask:

Does this reduce mental load?

Does this strengthen trust?

Does this make the customer's life easier?

Does this help someone important feel remembered?

If not, it doesn't belong in F.I. Forgot.
