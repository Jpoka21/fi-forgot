# Vision and Future Evolution

---

# Purpose

This document serves as the long term vision for F.I. Forgot.

Every previous document in this playbook explains how the product should be built today.

This document explains how the product should continue evolving over the next decade without losing its identity.

Technology will change.

Artificial intelligence will improve.

Interfaces will evolve.

Devices will disappear.

New forms of communication will emerge.

Relationships, however, will always matter.

Everything F.I. Forgot builds should strengthen meaningful human relationships.

That is the mission.

---

# Our Long Term Vision

F.I. Forgot exists to become the world's most trusted Relationship Concierge.

Not the world's best greeting card company.

Not the world's smartest AI assistant.

Not another productivity application.

Our vision is to build software that quietly helps people become more thoughtful spouses, parents, children, grandparents, siblings, friends, coworkers, clients, mentors, employees, employers, neighbors, and community members.

The greatest product success is not measured by cards mailed.

It is measured by relationships strengthened.

Responsible AI.

No manipulation.

# Product Decision Framework

Every future decision should answer these questions.

Does this strengthen relationships?

Does this reduce friction?

Does this increase thoughtfulness?

Does this respect user privacy?

Does this feel warm?

Does this reduce cognitive load?

Does it help users become better friends, family members, or professionals?

Would a world class Relationship Concierge do this?

If the answer is no, the feature should not exist.

Every victory.

Not by replacing love.

Every notification should have purpose.
