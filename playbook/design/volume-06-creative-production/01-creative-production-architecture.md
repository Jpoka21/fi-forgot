## 2. Constitutional Question

Volume 06 exists to answer one governing question:

> **Given frozen visual permission (Volume 02), governed surface implementation (Volume 03), contextual application policy (Volume 04), manufacturing constraints (Volume 01), and declared production intent, how shall visual artifacts be planned, realized, reviewed, approved as Governed Production-Ready, and handed off — without redefining upstream design law, contextual selection policy, permanent collection membership rules, manufacturing execution, Brain expression authority, or downstream library governance?**

This question is **production-transformational**. It governs the governed path from **declared production intent** to **Governed Production-Ready Artifact** — enclosed within upstream Compliance Boundaries and bounded before Volume 05 permanent library belonging begins.

- Whether a visual treatment is permitted in principle (Volume 02)

**Permanent principle:**

> **Existence → Governed Production-Ready → Permanent Membership → Fulfillment Execution are peer-separated constitutional transitions. None substitutes for another.**

### 5.9 Post-approval GPRA states

A **Governed Production-Ready Artifact (GPRA)** MAY transition to one of two peer-separated post-approval states. These are **not** forward lifecycle stages. They govern continued authority of a previously approved GPRA.

| State | Constitutional meaning | Prior approval validity when granted | New Governed Handoff permitted? | Downstream use of prior handoff |
|-------|------------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| **Invalidated** | The GPRA no longer satisfies governing law or required Compliance Boundaries | Historical fact of approval **is preserved**; approval was valid when granted | **No** — new Review and Approval REQUIRED before new GPRA | Existing downstream use is **not automatically revoked**; governed separately by Volume 05, engineering, and operational policy — but **new** intake or handoff MUST NOT proceed on Invalidated authority |
| **Superseded** | A newer authoritative GPRA has replaced this GPRA for a defined Production Obligation or Handoff Posture context | Historical fact of approval **is preserved**; prior approval was constitutionally valid when granted | **No** for the superseded GPRA; **Yes** for the replacing GPRA when otherwise eligible | Prior handoff authority **ceases for forward use** in the superseded context; replacement GPRA governs forward intake |

**Peer separation:**

> **Invalidated** means the GPRA fails current governing law. **Superseded** means a newer GPRA is authoritative for the same obligation or handoff context without asserting the earlier approval was invalid when granted.

Triggers include, but are not limited to: material upstream Compliance Boundary change (Invalidated); newer RVA version approved for the same Production Obligation (Superseded); governed Layer B succession rules (either state as applicable).

### 5.10 Declared Production Intent and Production Program cardinality

| Relationship | Constitutional default |
|--------------|------------------------|
| **Declared Production Intent → Production Program** | **1:1 by default** — one Declared Production Intent normally governs exactly one Production Program |
| **Governed split** | One Intent MAY yield **multiple** Production Programs only when scope separation is constitutionally necessary and recorded under governed Layer B or architecture rules — for example materially distinct realization scopes that cannot share one program boundary without authority ambiguity |
| **Multiple Intents → one Program** | **Prohibited by default** — one Production Program MUST NOT combine multiple unrelated Declared Production Intents unless a future governed standard explicitly authorizes consolidation and records the governing rationale |
| **Production Program → Production Obligation** | **1:N** — per Section 12 Stage Governance Matrix |

Every Production Program MUST remain traceable to exactly one Declared Production Intent. Every Production Obligation MUST belong to exactly one Production Program.

### 5.11 GPRA succession and Production Obligation authority

| Posture | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Historical GPRAs** | A Production Obligation MAY produce **multiple historically approved GPRAs** over time (for example successive RVA versions) |
| **Authoritative GPRA** | For a given Production Obligation and Handoff Posture consumer class, **only one GPRA is authoritative at a time** unless future Layer B law explicitly permits multiple simultaneously authoritative variants for distinct governed purposes |
| **Superseded relationship** | When a newer GPRA is approved for the same obligation and supersedes a prior GPRA in the same handoff context, the prior GPRA becomes **Superseded** per Section 5.9; the newer GPRA becomes the sole authoritative GPRA for that context |
| **Invalidated relationship** | A GPRA becomes **Invalidated** when governing law or feasibility boundaries fail, independent of whether a replacement GPRA yet exists |

**Constitutional default:**

> **Multiple historical GPRAs; one authoritative GPRA per obligation per handoff context.**

### 5.12 Shared artifact and multi-obligation use

| Posture | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Default** | One RVA or GPRA satisfying **more than one** Production Obligation is **not permitted by default** |
| **Permitted path** | Reuse across obligations is permitted **only through explicit obligation linkage** declared under governed Layer B rules — for example documented shared-source linkage where one realization outcome intentionally satisfies multiple bounded obligations |
| **Cross-collection reuse** | A GPRA presented for Volume 05 membership consideration in one collection **does not** create membership in another; collection-agnostic production **does not** imply automatic multi-collection membership |
| **Membership** | Reuse across obligations or collections **never** creates automatic Volume 05 permanent membership (AX-2, P3) |

| **Collection-Anchored Production Program** | Realization scope declared in service of a known governed collection's constitutional mandate | Collection exists; artifacts are not yet members |
| **Collection-Agnostic Production Program** | Realization scope not bound to a single governed collection | GPRAs may later be considered for one or more collections |

Both modes are constitutionally valid. Volume 06 MUST NOT require collection anchoring for all realization work.

### 10.4 Production Obligation

A **Production Obligation** is a bounded realization duty within a Production Program — for example a required visual treatment for a governed surface role, a variant within a series, or a licensed-intake slot.

A Production Obligation is **not** a template region definition (Volume 03), a collection entity (Volume 05), or an implementation slot identifier.

## 11. Primary Lifecycle

Volume 06 governs one primary lifecycle. Lifecycle stages are **constitutional states**, not implementation statuses, queue labels, or database values.

```
Declared Production Intent
        ↓
Production Obligation Definition
        ↓
Exploration
        ↓
Realization
        ↓
Review
        ↓
Approval
        ↓
Governed Handoff
```

### 11.1 Governed return and post-approval paths

**Rework** is not a separate forward lifecycle stage. It is a governed return path from Review to Realization:

> **Failed Review Determination** → Realization (same or successor Production Obligation)

> **Conditional pass with unsatisfied conditions** → Realization (same or successor Production Obligation) — conditions MUST be satisfied before Approval may proceed

**Revocation** is a post-approval transition governed in Domain 3 per Section 5.9:

> Governed Production-Ready Artifact → **Invalidated** or **Superseded** per Section 5.9 and Section 5.11

**Licensed or acquired intake** is not a separate lifecycle. Externally sourced visual material enters at Realization as an RVA candidate subject to the same Review and Approval path as created material (P8).

### 11.2 Lifecycle ownership relative to prior architecture

Volume 02 Section 11 Asset Lifecycle Ownership was extended through Harmonization Sprint H3; residual Volume 02 harmonization completed through H4.1 (Sections 3, 4.1, 9.1, 10, and related metadata). Volume 02 retains visual identity permission authority. Volume 06 owns realization and Governed Production-Ready Artifact approval. Volume 05 owns permanent collection membership intake of eligible GPRAs.

## 12. Stage Governance Matrix

Every constitutional stage MUST be interpretable through this matrix. Stages are **constitutional**; they are not implementation workflow steps.

| Stage | Input | Domain | Activity | Output | Cardinality | Exit conditions |
|-------|-------|--------|----------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Declared Production Intent** | Applicable Volumes 01–04 Compliance Boundaries knowable | 1 — Intent and Planning | Declare what visual outcome is sought and under what governing constraints | Declared Production Intent | 1 per program initiation | Intent is substantive, traceable, and bounded enough to define obligations |
| **Production Obligation Definition** | Declared Production Intent | 1 — Intent and Planning | Decompose intent into bounded realization duties within a Production Program | Production Obligation | 1:N per Production Program | Each obligation is bounded, constraint-bound, and realization-ready |
| **Exploration** | Production Obligation | 2 — Artifact Realization | Evaluate proposed directions before or during Realization commitment | Exploration Posture | 0:N; may be non-persistent | Sufficient direction exists to realize, or exploration is formally waived under governed rules |
| **Realization** | Production Obligation; Exploration exit | 2 — Artifact Realization | Bring visual artifact into governed existence under declared intent | Realized Visual Artifact (RVA) | 1:N versions per obligation | Visual artifact exists and is attributable to obligation |
| **Review** | Realized Visual Artifact (RVA) | 3 — Review, Approval, and Handoff | Evaluate RVA against Review dimensions including Design-Time Feasibility | Review Determination | 1 per RVA version reviewed | Pass, fail, or conditional determination recorded |
| **Approval** | Review Determination (pass, or conditional pass with all conditions satisfied) | 3 — Review, Approval, and Handoff | Authorize Governed Production-Ready status | Governed Production-Ready Artifact (GPRA) | 1 per approved RVA version per obligation | Approval authority exercised on documented grounds; supersedes prior authoritative GPRA for same obligation and handoff context per Section 5.11 |
| **Governed Handoff** | Governed Production-Ready Artifact (GPRA) | 3 — Review, Approval, and Handoff | Declare intake posture toward Volume 05 and/or production catalog consumers | Handoff Posture | 1 per GPRA per target consumer class | Handoff obligations satisfied; downstream intake may begin |

### 12.1 Review Determination outcomes

| Outcome | Constitutional effect |
|---------|-------------------------|
| **Pass** | RVA eligible for Approval |
| **Fail** | **Failed Review Determination** — rework return path to Realization per Section 11.1; no GPRA |
| **Conditional pass** | Approval permitted **only after** all documented conditions are satisfied; conditions MUST NOT narrow upstream Compliance Boundaries. If conditions remain unsatisfied, treat as rework return path to Realization per Section 11.1; no GPRA until conditions are satisfied and Review passes |

### 12.2 Handoff Posture consumer classes

| Consumer class | Constitutional meaning |
|----------------|------------------------|
| **Library intake posture** | GPRA presented for Volume 05 permanent membership consideration |
| **Production catalog posture** | GPRA authorized for production artwork library intake under engineering specification |

Handoff Posture classes MUST NOT be merged when their governing downstream rules differ.

## 13. Manufacturing Boundary

Volume 06 MUST distinguish four manufacturing-related concepts. Collapsing them causes constitutional and operational failure.

| Concept | Constitutional owner | Definition | What it is not |
|---------|---------------------|------------|----------------|
| **Design-Time Feasibility** | Volume 06 — Review dimension consuming `FI-MFG-*` Compliance Boundaries | The visual artifact, as designed, appears compatible with known manufacturing constraints | Proof of successful manufacture; fulfillment authorization |
| **Governed Production-Ready (GPRA)** | Volume 06 — Approval output | RVA passed all Review dimensions including Design-Time Feasibility; authorized for Governed Handoff | Permanent collection membership; Fulfillment-Ready |
| **Manufacturing Validation** | Engineering / operational validation bounded by Volume 01 policy | Pre-fulfillment checks that a specific artifact instance can be produced in operational systems | Volume 06 Approval; DAM workflow status |
| **Fulfillment Execution** | Volume 01 operational layer | Instance-level manufacture and shipment | Governed Production-Ready; membership |

### 13.1 Boundary rules

1. GPRA **requires** satisfied Design-Time Feasibility at Approval time.
2. GPRA does **not** require successful physical manufacture.
3. Manufacturing Validation **may not** substitute for Volume 06 Approval.
4. Manufacturing Validation **may block** Fulfillment Execution even when GPRA exists.
5. Volume 06 does **not** own Manufacturing Validation mechanics or Fulfillment Execution.
6. Manufacturing capability change follows Research Library and `FI-DSN-GOV-003` propagation before Design policy change; affected GPRAs MAY move to Invalidated via revocation posture.
