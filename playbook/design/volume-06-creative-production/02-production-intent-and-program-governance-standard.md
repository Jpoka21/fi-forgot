## 1. Purpose

This standard is the **normative governing foundation** for F.I. Forgot Volume 06 Domain 1 — Intent and Planning Authority under `CLS-CPR`.

It answers the governing question:

> How shall F.I. Forgot declare and govern Production Intent and the Production Program before artifact realization begins, including Production Obligations, applicable upstream Compliance Boundaries, exploration-entry authorization, and waiver posture?

This standard translates validated Intent and Planning architecture into enduring pre-realization governance policy. It does not replace frozen Volume 06 Creative Production Architecture, frozen upstream Volumes 01–04 standards, frozen `FI-DSN-GOV-004`, or deferred `FI-DSN-STD-013` through `FI-DSN-STD-015`.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Principal subject

This standard governs the **pre-realization decision structure** for:

- **Declared Production Intent** — what visual outcome is sought under frozen design law
- **Production Program** — the constitutional container for bounded realization work
- **Production Obligation** — bounded realization duties derived from intent within a program
- **Applicable upstream Compliance Boundaries** — identification and binding of frozen design-law limits
- **Exploration-entry authorization** — conditions under which Exploration Posture may be entered
- **Governed waiver posture** — how waivers, exceptions, unresolved constraints, and conditional obligations are recorded

### 2.2 In scope

- Decision-stage declaration and governance of Production Intent before Realization commitment
- Constitutional structure, modes, and boundaries of Production Programs
- Establishment, scope, and traceability of Production Obligations
- Identification and binding of applicable upstream Compliance Boundaries from Volumes 01–04
- Conditions required before Exploration Posture may be authorized
- Recording of waivers, exceptions, unresolved constraints, and conditional obligations
- Program Amendment and Intent Change posture affecting downstream realization eligibility
- Principal-subject placement and deferral relationships within Volume 06 Domain 1
- Constitutional distinctions between intent, program, obligation, exploration entry, and downstream lifecycle stages
- Manufacturing Compliance Boundary consumption without operational policy restatement

### 2.3 Out of scope

See Section 8.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Compliance Boundary** | The frozen set of normative limits from authoritative upstream standards within which a Production Program and its Production Obligations MUST remain; consumed per `FI-DSN-GOV-004` without restating upstream ownership |
| **Conditional Obligation** | A Production Obligation whose governed realization eligibility depends on satisfaction of one or more explicitly recorded constitutional conditions that remain unresolved at declaration time |
| **Declared Production Intent** | The authoritative Decision-stage statement of what visual outcome is sought and under what governing constraints — distinct from Brain message intent, a finished visual artifact, or Governed Production-Ready status |
| **Exception** | A documented, governed departure from a default constitutional rule within this standard's positive authority — distinct from an implementation workaround or runtime override |
| **Exploration-Entry Authorization** | The governed Decision-stage conclusion that a Production Obligation or Production Program MAY enter Exploration Posture under recorded conditions — distinct from GPRA approval, collection membership, or manufacturing authorization |
| **Intent Change** | A governed amendment to Declared Production Intent that MAY affect Production Program scope, Production Obligation validity, or downstream realization eligibility |
| **Production Intent** | Short form for **Declared Production Intent** when context is unambiguous within Volume 06 Domain 1 |
| **Production Obligation** | A bounded realization duty derived from Declared Production Intent within exactly one Production Program — distinct from a template region, collection member, or DAM task |
| **Production Program** | The constitutional container for a bounded body of realization work traceable to exactly one Declared Production Intent — distinct from a Signature Collection, product sprint, or engineering project |
| **Program Amendment** | A governed change to Production Program structure, scope, mode, or obligation set that is not a full Intent Change — MAY affect downstream realization eligibility per governed rules |
| **Unresolved Constraint** | An upstream Compliance Boundary condition, planning input, or constitutional prerequisite that is identified but not yet satisfied at the time of program governance evaluation — MUST be explicitly recorded and governed |
| **Waiver** | A governed recorded decision that a specified constitutional requirement, Compliance Boundary application, or exploration-entry precondition is temporarily or conditionally not enforced for a defined scope — distinct from Brain override or customer Selection |

## 4. Constitutional Distinctions

The following distinctions are **permanent** and govern interpretation of this standard:

| Distinction | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| Identity permission is not artifact existence | Volume 02 permission does not create a Realized Visual Artifact |
| Existence is not Approval | An RVA does not possess Governed Production-Ready authority |
| Approval is not membership | A GPRA does not grant permanent collection membership |
| Membership is not manufacturing validation | Volume 05 membership does not constitute Manufacturing Validation |
| Manufacturing validation is not fulfillment | Manufacturing Validation does not authorize Fulfillment Execution |
| Production Intent is not an artifact | Declared Production Intent governs sought outcomes; it is not an RVA or GPRA |
| A Production Program is not Approval | Program governance precedes and does not substitute for Review and Approval |
| Exploration-entry authorization is not permission to publish, admit, manufacture, or fulfill | Authorized exploration does not grant GPRA, membership, Manufacturing Validation, or Fulfillment authority |

## 9. Lifecycle and State Model

### 9.1 Conceptual program-governance states (draft candidates)

The following states describe **Domain 1 governance posture** for a Production Program. Labels are **draft candidates** pending requirement-sprint validation. They are not implementation enums, database fields, or workflow statuses.

| State (draft candidate) | Constitutional meaning |
|-------------------------|------------------------|
| **Intent Undeclared** | No governed Declared Production Intent exists for the program scope |
| **Intent Declared** | Declared Production Intent is recorded with minimum substance and traceability |
| **Program Drafted** | Production Program structure and obligation set are drafted but not yet fully governed |
| **Program Governed** | Production Program, obligations, and Compliance Boundary bindings satisfy Domain 1 governance requirements |
| **Conditionally Governed** | Program Governed with one or more recorded conditional obligations or unresolved constraints |
| **Exploration Entry Authorized** | Governed conclusion that Exploration Posture MAY be entered for a defined scope |
| **Exploration Entry Withheld** | Exploration Posture MUST NOT be entered until recorded preconditions are satisfied |
| **Program Amended** | Governed Program Amendment recorded; downstream eligibility evaluated per amendment rules |
| **Program Superseded** | A successor program or intent scope replaces this program for forward governance |
| **Program Invalidated** | Program no longer satisfies governing law or bound Compliance Boundaries |

### 9.2 Permissible transitions (conceptual)

```
Intent Undeclared → Intent Declared → Program Drafted → Program Governed
                                              ↓
                                   Conditionally Governed
                                              ↓
                          Exploration Entry Withheld ↔ Exploration Entry Authorized
                                              ↓
                                   Program Amended / Program Superseded / Program Invalidated
```

Realization, Review, Approval, and Handoff stages MUST NOT begin from **Intent Undeclared** or **Program Drafted** without reaching a governed Domain 1 posture that authorizes downstream work per future normative requirements.

## 10. Requirement Planning Architecture

Normative requirements (`FI-DSN-STD-012-R01` onward) are **deferred** to a subsequent requirement-drafting sprint. The following groups define the expected future requirement structure.

### G1 — Constitutional inheritance and scope

Future requirements will establish non-contradiction with frozen Volume 06 architecture P1–P11, reconcilability with upstream Volumes 01–04 Compliance Boundaries, and Decision-stage-only scope boundaries excluding implementation vocabulary.

### G2 — Production Intent declaration

Future requirements will govern minimum substance, traceability, boundedness, upstream alignment, and distinction from Brain message intent and collection mandates.

### G3 — Production Program structure

Future requirements will govern program container rules, Intent-to-Program cardinality (default 1:1), governed split posture, program modes, and traceability to exactly one Declared Production Intent.

### G4 — Production Obligations

Future requirements will govern obligation decomposition, scope boundaries, 1:N Program-to-Obligation cardinality, obligation traceability, and prohibition on obligation definitions that smuggle realization or approval authority.

### G5 — Compliance Boundary binding

Future requirements will govern identification of applicable `FI-DSN-STD-001`–`009`, `FI-MFG-*`, and volume architecture inputs; binding without restatement; and response when boundaries conflict with declared intent.

### G6 — Exploration-entry authorization

Future requirements will govern preconditions for Exploration Posture entry, withholding posture, and distinction from GPRA, membership, and manufacturing authorization.

### G7 — Waiver and exception governance

Future requirements will govern recording, scope, authority, duration, and effect of waivers, exceptions, unresolved constraints, and conditional obligations.

### G8 — Change, amendment, and invalidation

Future requirements will govern Intent Change, Program Amendment, Program Superseded, Program Invalidated, and effects on downstream realization eligibility without prescribing implementation notification mechanics.

### G9 — Auditability and evidence

Future requirements will govern what constitutional records MUST exist for intent, program, obligation, boundary binding, and waiver decisions — without inventing metadata schemas owned by `FI-DSN-GOV-002`.

### G10 — Authority exclusions and downstream handoff

Future requirements will govern explicit deferral to `FI-DSN-STD-013` and record the Domain 1 outputs that Artifact Realization MAY consume without transferring Domain 1 ownership.

## 11. Brain Interaction

Brain Architecture and the Writing Engine MAY inform Declared Production Intent. They MUST NOT:

- declare or substitute for governed Production Intent
- authorize Exploration Posture, GPRA status, or Governed Handoff
- waive Compliance Boundaries or Production Obligation preconditions
- grant collection membership or manufacturing authority

Normative Brain Interaction requirements are deferred to the requirement-drafting sprint per `FI-DSN-TPL-001` Section 7.
