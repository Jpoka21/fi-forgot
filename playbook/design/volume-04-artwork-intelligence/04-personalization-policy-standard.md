**Standard statement:** F.I. Forgot maintains **one authoritative Personalization Policy** standard that governs Decision-stage customer-specific and relationship-specific visual treatment boundaries for governed design decisions, and the personalization policy outputs peer standards may consume — without governing contextual semantics, authorized selection, context-bound eligibility, metadata field semantics, relationship records, message content, Brain runtime mechanics, or manufacturing operational policy.

**Source basis:** Company judgment per `FI-DSN-GOV-003`. Applicable frozen `FI-MFG-*` obligations are consumed as Compliance Boundary inputs only. This standard is not derived from Brain runtime behavior, product implementation, customer profile systems, or vendor facts.

## 1. Purpose

This standard is the **normative governing foundation** for F.I. Forgot Volume 04 Domain 2 — Personalization Policy under `CLS-PER`.

It answers the locked governing question:

> What customer- or relationship-specific visual treatment boundaries are permitted or prohibited for governed design decisions, and what personalization policy outputs may peer standards consume?

This standard translates validated Personalization Policy architecture into enduring Decision-stage personalization policy. It does not replace frozen Volume 04 Artwork Intelligence Architecture, frozen `FI-DSN-GOV-004`, frozen `FI-DSN-STD-007`, frozen `FI-DSN-STD-008`, or frozen upstream Volume 02 and Volume 03 standards.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In scope

- Decision-stage customer-specific and relationship-specific visual treatment policy
- Personalization treatment boundaries — permit and prohibit rules under personalization policy
- Personalization policy output posture for consumption by peer Volume 04 standards
- Semantic consumption boundaries from frozen `FI-DSN-STD-008` without semantic redefinition
- Principal-subject placement and deferral relationships
- Cross-surface personalization policy applicability under upstream Volume 03 constraints
- Brain Interaction policy boundary recording per `FI-DSN-TPL-001` Section 7
- Manufacturing Compliance Boundary integration without operational policy restatement

### 2.2 Out of scope

See Section 11.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Customer-specific treatment policy** | Decision-stage rules governing how customer-specific attributes may permit or prohibit visual treatments in governed design — distinct from customer profile storage, metadata field definitions, and selection authorization |
| **Decision-stage personalization policy** | Frozen normative personalization boundary rules recorded in Design Standards; not runtime adaptation, message wording, or authorized alternative sets |
| **Personalization Policy decision** | A governed design decision whose principal normative subject is customer-specific or relationship-specific visual treatment boundaries, or personalization policy outputs under `CLS-PER` |
| **Personalization policy output** | A governed treatment-boundary or policy artifact produced by this standard for consumption by peer standards without transferring policy ownership |
| **Personalization treatment boundary** | A Decision-stage rule defining which visual treatments are permitted or prohibited under customer-specific or relationship-specific personalization policy — distinct from visual permission (Volume 02), contextual semantics (`CLS-OEC`), spatial placement (Volume 03), and authorized alternative sets (`CLS-BVS`) |
| **Relationship-specific treatment policy** | Decision-stage rules governing how relationship-specific attributes may permit or prohibit visual treatments in governed design — distinct from relationship-emotion semantics (`CLS-OEC`), message copy, and relationship record storage |

## 5. Governing Requirements

### 5.1 Principal-subject placement

A decision belongs to Personalization Policy when customer-specific or relationship-specific visual treatment boundaries, or personalization policy outputs, is the **principal normative subject**.

| Step | Question | If yes → |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Is customer-specific or relationship-specific visual treatment boundary policy, or personalization policy outputs, the principal subject? | Candidate `CLS-PER` / Domain 2 |
| 2 | Is occasion or emotional context **meaning** for design the principal subject? | Defer to `FI-DSN-STD-008` — `CLS-OEC` |
| 3 | Is whether a permitted treatment may appear in an authorized alternative set the principal subject? | Defer to `FI-DSN-STD-007` — `CLS-BVS` |
| 4 | Is visual identity, permission, or global exclusion the principal subject? | Volume 02 |
| 5 | Is surface structure, spatial allocation, or exterior geometry the principal subject? | Volume 03 — `FI-DSN-STD-004`–`006` |
| 6 | Is message content or copywriting the principal subject? | Brain Architecture / product |
| 7 | Is metadata field definition, schema, or dictionary semantics the principal subject? | `FI-DSN-GOV-002` |
| 8 | Is customer profile storage, CRM identity, or relationship record ownership the principal subject? | Product / engineering — not Volume 04 |

When steps conflict, **principal normative subject** per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` Section 14.1 governs.

#### Normative requirements — Principal-subject placement

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-009-R05` | Personalization Policy SHALL govern Personalization Policy decisions whose principal subject is customer-specific visual treatment boundaries, relationship-specific visual treatment boundaries, or personalization policy outputs under `CLS-PER`. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-009-R06` | Personalization Policy SHALL defer authority for the following subjects to their authoritative owners when those subjects are principal: occasion and emotional context semantics, related contextual dimension meaning, and representational boundaries (`FI-DSN-STD-008`); context-bound eligibility and authorized alternatives (`FI-DSN-STD-007`); Preference Surfaces and customer override application for visual selection (`FI-DSN-STD-007`; `FI-DSN-GOV-004`); metadata field semantics and schemas (`FI-DSN-GOV-002`); visual permission and identity-level eligibility (Volume 02); surface system structure, spatial allocation, safe areas, bleed integration, and exterior presentation geometry (`FI-DSN-STD-004`, `FI-DSN-STD-005`, `FI-DSN-STD-006`); collection membership, release, and retirement (Volume 05); manufacturing operational policy (`FI-MFG-*`); message content and copywriting (Brain Architecture / product); customer profile storage, CRM identity, and relationship record ownership (product / engineering); and Brain runtime behavior, ranking, and recommendation mechanics (`FI-DSN-GOV-004` runtime layer). | Company judgment |

## 8. Brain Interaction

Personalization Policy defines Decision-stage treatment boundaries and policy outputs. Brain Runtime MAY use customer- or relationship-specific signals when operating within bounds established by frozen `FI-DSN-STD-007` and upstream Compliance Boundaries.

Brain Runtime SHALL NOT:

- redefine customer-specific or relationship-specific treatment boundaries as normative policy
- treat runtime inference, telemetry, customer profile history, or recommendation history as sources of Decision-stage personalization policy
- authorize visual alternatives, widen Preference Surfaces, or alter context-bound eligibility based on personalization inference alone
- substitute message wording or Writing Engine output for governed personalization boundaries

#### Normative requirements — Brain Interaction

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-009-R20` | Brain Runtime use of customer- or relationship-specific signals affecting governed visual design SHALL remain reconcilable with frozen personalization treatment boundaries and policy outputs established by this standard. Brain Runtime SHALL NOT redefine customer-specific or relationship-specific treatment boundaries as Decision-stage policy. Brain Runtime SHALL NOT treat runtime inference, telemetry, customer profile history, repeated narrowing patterns, Brain recommendation history, or adaptive runtime patterns as sources of Decision-stage personalization policy. Brain Runtime SHALL NOT authorize visual alternatives, widen Preference Surfaces, or alter context-bound eligibility based on personalization inference alone. Brain Runtime SHALL NOT substitute message wording or Writing Engine output for governed personalization boundaries. | Company judgment |
