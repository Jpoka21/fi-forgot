## 1. Purpose

This document is the **Volume Governance architecture** for F.I. Forgot Volume 04 — Artwork Intelligence.

Its purpose is to define:

- The permanent constitutional role of contextual visual application within the Design Library
- The governing question Volume 04 answers after Volumes 02 and 03 are complete
- What authorities exist within Volume 04's three constitutional domains and what they do not own
- How future Volume 04 standards are assigned to domains and classifications
- How Volume 04 inherits upstream visual permission and surface implementation authority
- How Volume 04 relates to peer volumes and frozen governance artifacts

This document is **not** an occasion taxonomy, personalization workflow, artwork eligibility checklist, Preference Surface catalog, metadata schema, collection governance document, manufacturing standard, engineering specification, product UI guide, or Brain implementation document. Those belong to future standards or systems governed by this architecture.

Product contexts may expand. Brain capabilities may grow. Rendering technology may change. This architecture SHALL remain stable across those changes.

## 2. Constitutional Question

Volume 04 exists to answer one governing question:

> **Given permitted visual treatments (Volume 02) and governed surface implementation (Volume 03), which permitted visual treatment applies in a given contextual circumstance under governed Decision-stage policy, without modifying upstream visual permission, structural authority, or downstream library governance?**

This question is **context-binding**. It governs instance-level visual application — binding permitted treatments to occasion, relationship, emotional context, and personalization circumstances — enclosed within upstream Compliance Boundaries.

Volume 04 does **not** answer:

- Whether a treatment is visually permitted in principle (Volume 02)
- Where a treatment may occupy a governed surface (Volume 03)
- How asset libraries operate (Volume 05)
- What message wording is expressed (Brain Architecture / product)
- How the Brain ranks or computes recommendations (runtime implementation)

## 8. Constitutional Domains

Volume 04 is organized into **three constitutional domains**. Each domain answers an independent governing sub-question. Each domain maps to one future Layer B standard and one primary `CLS-*` classification.

**Domain numbering versus drafting sequence:** Constitutional domain numbers (1–3) express **organizational and principal-subject structure** — dependency relationships and governing sub-questions. Domain order does **not** prescribe Layer B drafting order, implementation order, or runtime evaluation order. Drafting sequence is governed separately in **Section 11**.

### 8.1 Domain 1 — Context Semantics

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Governing sub-question** | What do occasion, emotional intent, and related contextual dimensions mean for design decisions, and what representational boundaries do they impose? |
| **Primary Classification** | `CLS-OEC` — Occasion and Emotional Context |
| **Candidate standard** | Occasion and Emotional Context Standard |
| **Reserved Standard ID** | `FI-DSN-STD-008` (per `FI-DSN-REG-001`) |
| **Does not own** | Brain recommendation mechanics, selection authorization, personalization policy, message wording, surface structure |

### 8.2 Domain 2 — Personalization Policy

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Governing sub-question** | What customer- or relationship-specific visual treatment boundaries are permitted or prohibited? |
| **Primary Classification** | `CLS-PER` — Personalization |
| **Candidate standard** | Personalization Policy Standard |
| **Reserved Standard ID** | `FI-DSN-STD-009` (per `FI-DSN-REG-001`) |
| **Does not own** | Visual source provenance semantics (`FI-DSN-GOV-002`), Brain selection constraints when selection is principal (`CLS-BVS`), surface placement |

### 8.3 Domain 3 — Authorized Selection

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Governing sub-question** | Among permitted, structurally valid treatments, which alternatives may appear on Preference Surfaces, and what override classes apply? |
| **Primary Classification** | `CLS-BVS` — Brain Visual Selection |
| **Candidate standard** | Brain Visual Selection Standard |
| **Reserved Standard ID** | `FI-DSN-STD-007` (per `FI-DSN-REG-001`) |
| **Does not own** | Occasion semantics (`CLS-OEC`), personalization policy (`CLS-PER`), Brain execution logic, ranking algorithms |

### 8.4 Domain dependency model

```
Domain 1 — Context Semantics (CLS-OEC)
Domain 2 — Personalization Policy (CLS-PER)
        ↓ constrain contextual inputs
Domain 3 — Authorized Selection (CLS-BVS)
        ↓ enclosed within
Upstream Compliance Boundaries (Volumes 02, 03, FI-MFG-*)
```

Domains 1 and 2 are **peer inputs** to Domain 3. Neither Domain 1 nor Domain 2 is subordinate to the other. Domain 3 MUST NOT be authored as if occasion semantics or personalization policy were implicit.

### 8.5 Cross-surface applicability

All three domains apply to contextual visual application on **card surfaces and exterior surfaces** alike, subject to frozen Volume 03 structural, spatial, and `CLS-EEP` constraints. Volume 04 MUST NOT create a separate constitutional domain for exterior selection. Exterior contextual application is governed within these three domains under exterior Compliance Boundaries established by `FI-DSN-STD-006`.

### 8.6 Consolidation posture

The three-domain model is the **minimum constitutional architecture** validated through governed domain elimination, merge, and relocation review per `FI-DSN-CLS-001`. Mergers among domains (BVS+OEC, BVS+PER, OEC+PER) or a single mega-standard are **rejected** at the constitutional layer because each domain answers an independent governing question with distinct principal-subject ownership.
