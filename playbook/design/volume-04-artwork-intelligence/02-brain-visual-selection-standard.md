**Standard statement:** F.I. Forgot maintains **one authoritative Brain Visual Selection** standard that governs Decision-stage authorization of permitted visual alternatives on contextual Preference Surfaces, enclosure within upstream Compliance Boundaries, customer override specialization, and context-bound eligibility for visual selection — without governing Brain runtime mechanics, ranking, occasion semantics, personalization policy, metadata field semantics, product workflow, or manufacturing operational policy.

**Source basis:** Company judgment per `FI-DSN-GOV-003`. Applicable frozen `FI-MFG-*` obligations are consumed as Compliance Boundary inputs only. This standard is not derived from Brain runtime behavior, product implementation, or vendor facts.

---

## 1. Purpose

This standard is the **normative governing foundation** for F.I. Forgot Volume 04 Domain 3 — Authorized Selection under `CLS-BVS`.

It answers the approved governing question:

> Among visual treatments already permitted by Volume 02 and structurally valid under Volume 03, which alternatives may be authorized on governed Preference Surfaces for contextual visual application, under what Decision-stage policy, enclosed within upstream Compliance Boundaries — without governing runtime execution, algorithms, ranking, UI behavior, occasion semantics, personalization policy, or metadata ownership?

This standard translates validated Brain Visual Selection architecture into enduring Decision-stage selection policy. It does not replace frozen Volume 04 Artwork Intelligence Architecture, frozen `FI-DSN-GOV-004`, or frozen upstream Volume 02 and Volume 03 standards.

### 2.1 In scope

- Compliance Boundary consumption and Preference Surface enclosure

See Section 11.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Authorized alternative** | A permitted visual treatment explicitly authorized on a governed contextual Preference Surface at the Decision stage |
| **Brain Visual Selection decision** | A governed design decision whose principal normative subject is authorized visual alternatives, contextual Preference Surface boundaries, or customer override application for contextual visual selection under `CLS-BVS` |
| **Compliance Boundary** | The frozen set of normative limits within which all authorized alternatives and Preference Surfaces MUST remain per `FI-DSN-GOV-004` Section 7.1 |
| **Context-bound eligibility** | Whether a permitted treatment may be included in an authorized alternative set for a given selection context — distinct from identity-level eligibility and from occasion semantics when principal in `CLS-OEC` |
| **Contextual Preference Surface** | A governed Decision-stage set of authorized visual alternatives for contextual visual application when `CLS-BVS` is principal — distinct from spatial Preference Surfaces when principal in Volume 03 |
| **Decision-stage policy** | Frozen normative authorization recorded in Design Standards; not runtime behavior, instance selection mechanics, or product workflow |
| **Selection-input consumption** | Use of upstream contextual inputs produced by peer standards or metadata governance without owning their semantics |

## 5. Governing Requirements

### 5.1 Principal-subject placement

A decision belongs to Brain Visual Selection when authorized visual alternatives, contextual Preference Surface boundaries, or customer override application for contextual visual selection is the **principal normative subject**.

| Step | Question | If yes → |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Is authorized visual selection or contextual Preference Surface authorization the principal subject? | Candidate `CLS-BVS` / Domain 3 |
| 2 | Is occasion or emotional context **meaning** for design the principal subject? | Defer to `FI-DSN-STD-008` — `CLS-OEC` |
| 3 | Is personalization treatment policy the principal subject? | Defer to `FI-DSN-STD-009` — `CLS-PER` |
| 4 | Is visual identity or exclusion the principal subject? | Volume 02 |
| 5 | Is surface structure, spatial allocation, or exterior geometry the principal subject? | Volume 03 — `FI-DSN-STD-004`–`006` |

When steps conflict, **principal normative subject** per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` Section 14.1 governs.

#### Normative requirements — Principal-subject placement

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-007-R05` | Brain Visual Selection SHALL govern Brain Visual Selection decisions whose principal subject is authorized visual alternatives, contextual Preference Surface boundaries, or customer override application for contextual visual selection under `CLS-BVS`. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-007-R06` | Brain Visual Selection SHALL defer authority for the following subjects to their authoritative owners when those subjects are principal: visual permission and identity-level eligibility (Volume 02); surface system structure, spatial allocation, safe areas, bleed integration, and exterior presentation geometry (`FI-DSN-STD-004`, `FI-DSN-STD-005`, `FI-DSN-STD-006`); occasion and emotional context semantics (`FI-DSN-STD-008`); personalization policy (`FI-DSN-STD-009`); metadata field semantics and schemas (`FI-DSN-GOV-002`); collection membership, release, and retirement (Volume 05); manufacturing operational policy (`FI-MFG-*`); message content and copywriting (Brain Architecture / product); and Brain runtime behavior, ranking, and recommendation mechanics (`FI-DSN-GOV-004` runtime layer). | Company judgment |

## 7. Company Judgment

This standard is based on company judgment.

F.I. Forgot has chosen to govern Brain Visual Selection because frozen Volume 04 architecture assigns authorized selection and contextual Preference Surface authorization to Domain 3 under `CLS-BVS`, and because Decision-stage selection policy must be frozen independently of Brain runtime mechanics, occasion semantics, and personalization policy. That separation is not derived from Brain runtime behavior or vendor documentation.

Frozen upstream Volume 02 and Volume 03 standards, frozen `FI-DSN-GOV-004`, and applicable frozen `FI-MFG-*` obligations informed drafting as Compliance Boundary inputs only. `FI-DSN-STD-008` and `FI-DSN-STD-009` remain deferred semantic owners for peer inputs.

## 8. Brain Interaction

Brain Visual Selection defines Compliance Boundaries and authorized contextual Preference Surfaces for governed visual selection. Brain Runtime MAY recommend, rank within bounds, and select only within frozen Preference Surfaces completely enclosed in the governing Compliance Boundary.

Brain recommendations remain advisory until Selection within bounds per `FI-DSN-GOV-004` Section 8. Runtime narrowing of a Preference Surface is operational only and SHALL NOT amend frozen Decision-stage authorization.

Brain Runtime SHALL NOT:

- widen contextual Preference Surfaces or Compliance Boundaries
- authorize alternatives outside frozen Volume 02 permission or Volume 03 structural, spatial, or exterior limits
- override applicable manufacturing Compliance Boundaries
- redefine occasion semantics, personalization policy, or metadata field semantics
- prescribe or encode ranking algorithms, scoring models, or default-selection mechanics as normative policy

#### Normative requirements — Brain Interaction

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-007-R20` | Brain Runtime recommendations, customer selections, and customer overrides affecting governed visual selection SHALL remain within frozen Compliance Boundaries and authorized contextual Preference Surfaces established by this standard and applicable upstream standards. Brain Runtime SHALL NOT widen Preference Surfaces, narrow Compliance Boundaries permanently, invent unauthorized visual alternatives, override applicable manufacturing Compliance Boundaries, redefine upstream Volume 02 or Volume 03 authority, occasion semantics, personalization policy, metadata field semantics, or treat recommendation, ranking, or selection behavior as Decision-stage policy. | Company judgment |
