**Standard statement:** F.I. Forgot maintains **one authoritative Occasion and Emotional Context** standard that governs Decision-stage semantic meaning of occasion, emotional intent, and related contextual dimensions for governed design decisions, and the representational boundaries those dimensions impose — without governing authorized selection, context-bound eligibility, personalization policy, message content, metadata field semantics, visual permission, surface structure, Brain runtime mechanics, or manufacturing operational policy.

**Source basis:** Company judgment per `FI-DSN-GOV-003`. Applicable frozen `FI-MFG-*` obligations are consumed as Compliance Boundary inputs only. This standard is not derived from Brain runtime behavior, product implementation, or vendor facts.

---

## 1. Purpose

This standard is the **normative governing foundation** for F.I. Forgot Volume 04 Domain 1 — Context Semantics under `CLS-OEC`.

It answers the locked governing question:

> What do occasion, emotional intent, and related contextual dimensions mean for governed design decisions, and what representational boundaries do they impose?

This standard translates validated Occasion and Emotional Context architecture into enduring Decision-stage semantic policy. It does not replace frozen Volume 04 Artwork Intelligence Architecture, frozen `FI-DSN-GOV-004`, frozen `FI-DSN-STD-007`, or frozen upstream Volume 02 and Volume 03 standards.

---

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In scope

- Decision-stage semantic meaning of occasion, emotional intent, and related contextual dimensions for governed design decisions
- Representational boundaries imposed by Domain 1 semantics
- Semantic interpretation policy at the Decision stage
- Semantic output posture for consumption by peer Volume 04 standards
- Principal-subject placement and deferral relationships
- Cross-surface semantic applicability under upstream Volume 03 constraints
- Brain Interaction semantic boundary recording per `FI-DSN-TPL-001` Section 7
- Manufacturing Compliance Boundary integration without operational policy restatement

### 2.2 Out of scope

See Section 11.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Context Semantics decision** | A governed design decision whose principal normative subject is occasion meaning, emotional intent meaning, related contextual dimension meaning, or representational boundaries imposed by those dimensions under `CLS-OEC` |
| **Decision-stage semantic policy** | Frozen normative meaning and representational boundary rules recorded in Design Standards; not runtime inference, message wording, or selection authorization |
| **Emotional intent (design)** | The governed design-relevant dimension of emotional meaning that `CLS-OEC` defines and bounds — distinct from message copy, Writing Engine tone, and personalization treatment policy |
| **Occasion meaning (design)** | The governed design-relevant semantics of occasion types, occasion context, and occasion-related contextual dimensions — distinct from occasion metadata field definitions and runtime occasion detection |
| **Related contextual dimension** | A governed contextual attribute that constrains design meaning alongside occasion and emotional intent when principal in Domain 1 — not a substitute for personalization policy, selection authorization, or Volume 03 structure |
| **Representational boundary** | A Decision-stage rule defining how occasion, emotional intent, or related contextual dimensions may or may not be represented in governed visual design — distinct from visual permission (Volume 02), spatial placement (Volume 03), and authorized alternative sets (`CLS-BVS`) |
| **Semantic output** | A governed contextual meaning or representational boundary artifact produced by this standard for consumption by peer standards without transferring semantic ownership |

## 5. Governing Requirements

### 5.1 Principal-subject placement

A decision belongs to Occasion and Emotional Context when occasion meaning, emotional intent meaning, related contextual dimension meaning, or representational boundaries imposed by those dimensions is the **principal normative subject**.

| Step | Question | If yes → |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Is occasion meaning, emotional intent meaning, related contextual dimension meaning, or representational boundaries imposed by those dimensions the principal subject? | Candidate `CLS-OEC` / Domain 1 |
| 2 | Is whether a permitted treatment may appear in an authorized alternative set the principal subject? | Defer to `FI-DSN-STD-007` — `CLS-BVS` |
| 3 | Is personalization treatment policy the principal subject? | Defer to `FI-DSN-STD-009` — `CLS-PER` |
| 4 | Is visual identity, permission, or global exclusion the principal subject? | Volume 02 |
| 5 | Is surface structure, spatial allocation, or exterior geometry the principal subject? | Volume 03 — `FI-DSN-STD-004`–`006` |
| 6 | Is message content or copywriting the principal subject? | Brain Architecture / product |

When steps conflict, **principal normative subject** per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` Section 14.1 governs.

#### Normative requirements — Principal-subject placement

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-008-R05` | Occasion and Emotional Context SHALL govern Context Semantics decisions whose principal subject is occasion meaning, emotional intent meaning, related contextual dimension meaning, or representational boundaries imposed by those dimensions under `CLS-OEC`. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-008-R06` | Occasion and Emotional Context SHALL defer authority for the following subjects to their authoritative owners when those subjects are principal: context-bound eligibility and authorized alternatives (`FI-DSN-STD-007`); Preference Surfaces and customer override application for visual selection (`FI-DSN-STD-007`; `FI-DSN-GOV-004`); personalization policy (`FI-DSN-STD-009`); metadata field semantics and schemas (`FI-DSN-GOV-002`); visual permission and identity-level eligibility (Volume 02); surface system structure, spatial allocation, safe areas, bleed integration, and exterior presentation geometry (`FI-DSN-STD-004`, `FI-DSN-STD-005`, `FI-DSN-STD-006`); collection membership, release, and retirement (Volume 05); manufacturing operational policy (`FI-MFG-*`); message content and copywriting (Brain Architecture / product); and Brain runtime behavior, ranking, and recommendation mechanics (`FI-DSN-GOV-004` runtime layer). | Company judgment |

## 7. Company Judgment

This standard is based on company judgment.

F.I. Forgot has chosen to govern Occasion and Emotional Context because frozen Volume 04 architecture assigns occasion and emotional semantics for design to Domain 1 under `CLS-OEC`, and because semantic meaning must be frozen independently of authorized selection, personalization policy, message content, and Brain runtime mechanics.

Frozen upstream Volume 02 and Volume 03 standards, frozen `FI-DSN-GOV-004`, frozen `FI-DSN-STD-007`, and applicable frozen `FI-MFG-*` obligations informed drafting as constraint and handoff context only. `FI-DSN-STD-009` remains the deferred personalization policy owner.

## 8. Brain Interaction

Occasion and Emotional Context defines Decision-stage semantic meaning and representational boundaries. Brain Runtime MAY use occasion and emotional signals when operating within bounds established by frozen `FI-DSN-STD-007` and upstream Compliance Boundaries.

Brain Runtime SHALL NOT:

- redefine occasion or emotional semantics as normative policy
- treat runtime inference, telemetry, or recommendation history as sources of Decision-stage semantic policy
- authorize visual alternatives or widen Preference Surfaces based on occasion or emotional inference alone
- substitute message wording or Writing Engine output for governed semantic boundaries

#### Normative requirements — Brain Interaction

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-008-R22` | Brain Runtime use of occasion, emotional intent, and related contextual signals affecting governed visual design SHALL remain reconcilable with frozen semantic meaning and representational boundaries established by this standard. Brain Runtime SHALL NOT redefine occasion meaning, emotional intent meaning, related contextual dimension meaning, or representational boundaries as Decision-stage policy. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-008-R23` | Brain Runtime SHALL NOT treat runtime inference, telemetry, repeated narrowing patterns, Brain recommendation history, or adaptive runtime patterns as sources of Decision-stage semantic policy. Brain Runtime SHALL NOT authorize visual alternatives, widen Preference Surfaces, or alter context-bound eligibility based on occasion or emotional inference alone. | Company judgment |
