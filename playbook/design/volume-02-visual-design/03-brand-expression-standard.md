**Standard statement:** F.I. Forgot maintains **one authoritative Brand Expression** standard that defines how identity-bound brand presentation is governed, how brand expression decisions are evaluated, and how Layer 2 constrains downstream visual standards without absorbing their authority. This standard populates Layer 2 — Brand Expression. It does not define logos, colors, typography, illustration, photography, composition rules, surface layout, artwork selection, collection operations, or implementation behavior.

**Source basis:** Company judgment per `FI-DSN-GOV-003`. This standard is not derived from vendor facts, verified evidence, or Brain runtime behavior.

---

## 1. Purpose

This standard is the **normative governing foundation** for F.I. Forgot Volume 02 Layer 2 — Brand Expression.

It translates approved Layer 2 architecture into enduring identity-bound presentation commitments and judgment discipline that downstream standards in Element Systems, Composition, Visual Boundaries, and peer volumes must remain reconcilable with.

This standard does not replace frozen Volume 02 Visual Design Architecture or `FI-DSN-PRN-001`. It operates within that architecture and remains subordinate to Layer 1 philosophical authority.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Identity-bound presentation** | A governed visual decision whose principal normative subject is whether a treatment is permitted, required, or prohibited **because of F.I. Forgot brand identity or brand-consistent presentation discipline**, rather than because of reusable element-domain rules, compositional relationships, surface layout, contextual selection, or global exclusions alone |
| **Brand boundary discipline** | Identity-level rules governing what F.I. Forgot brand presentation may and may not do — distinct from Layer 5 global visual exclusions |
| **Brand presentation posture** | Identity-level presentation character governed at Layer 2 — how the brand presents itself as a coherent identity, not how elements are spatially arranged on a surface |

## 5. Purpose Architecture

Brand expression governs **identity-bound presentation decisions** for F.I. Forgot. It translates relationship-centered visual philosophy into identity-bound presentation discipline without becoming a style guide, asset manual, or implementation specification.

Philosophy explains why governed visuals exist. Brand expression governs what F.I. Forgot brand presentation may do at the identity boundary.

### 5.1 What Brand Expression permanently governs

Brand Expression permanently governs four constitutional subjects:

| Subject | Enduring governing role |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Identity-bound presentation authority** | Which decisions belong to Layer 2 because brand identity or brand-consistent discipline is principal |
| **Brand boundary discipline** | Identity-level permitted, required, and prohibited presentation — distinct from Layer 5 global exclusions |
| **Brand presentation posture** | Identity-level presentation character — not compositional arrangement or surface layout |
| **Downstream constraint posture** | How Layer 2 bounds downstream standards without absorbing their domains |

These subjects are enduring. They SHALL remain valid across branding refresh, new media, new products, AI-generated imagery, new manufacturers, and future presentation technologies.

### 5.2 Identity-bound presentation authority

A decision is identity-bound when removing F.I. Forgot brand identity from the decision would change the normative answer.

**Placement discipline** — authors SHALL apply the following test to determine whether `CLS-BEX` is the appropriate principal classification. This test operationalizes `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1; it does not replace principal-subject law.

| Step | Question | If yes → |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Would the normative answer change if F.I. Forgot brand identity were removed from the decision? | Candidate for `CLS-BEX` / Layer 2 |
| 2 | Is the principal subject a reusable element type's character or system? | Layer 3 — applicable `CLS-TYP`, `CLS-COL`, `CLS-ILL`, `CLS-PHO`, or `CLS-ART` |
| 3 | Is the principal subject spatial or relational discipline among elements? | Layer 4 — `CLS-CMP` when relational |
| 4 | Is the principal subject a prohibition or minimum regardless of brand intent? | Layer 5 |
| 5 | Is the principal subject occasion fit, selection, or personalization policy? | Volume 04 |

When steps conflict, **principal normative subject** per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1 governs.

*Illustrative note (non-normative):* Volume 02 §13.1 assigns identity-level presentation posture — for example, a stated brand posture toward visual density — to Layer 2, and relational presentation among elements to Layer 4. The illustration demonstrates placement only; it does not prescribe any identity posture for F.I. Forgot.

### 5.3 Brand boundary discipline

Brand boundary discipline governs identity-level presentation limits. It answers whether a treatment is permitted, required, or prohibited **because of F.I. Forgot brand identity**.

Consistent brand boundary discipline implies that identity-bound rules do not contradict one another across governed artifact classes where the same brand identity applies. This identity-bound coherence is a consequence of boundary discipline — not collection governance, library operations, or implementation-level consistency.

Brand boundary discipline is **not** Layer 5 global exclusion. A treatment may be identity-breaking without being globally prohibited, and globally prohibited treatments remain prohibited regardless of brand preference.

### 5.4 Brand presentation posture

Brand presentation posture is identity-level presentation character — how F.I. Forgot presents itself as a coherent brand across governed artifacts.

Brand presentation posture is a **category of Layer 2 decision**. It does not prescribe aesthetic taste, marketing positioning, or style-guide conclusions.

Brand presentation posture does **not** govern:

- Spatial hierarchy, balance, prominence, or density among elements (Layer 4)
- Template regions, safe areas, or surface coordinates (Volume 03)
- Executable mark, color, type, or artwork specifications (downstream standards within or below Layer 2)

### 5.5 Downstream constraint posture

Layer 2 constrains Layer 3 and downstream volumes per Volume 02 §7.1. Element systems operate within brand boundaries. Brand expression defines those boundaries; it does not author element systems, composition rules, surface layout, contextual selection, or collection operations.

Downstream constraint is **binding authority**, not implementation guidance.

## 6. Decision Architecture

### 6.1 Stacked evaluation procedure

Brand expression decisions SHALL be evaluated through a **stacked evaluation procedure**. Each gate is sequential. Failure at any gate renders the decision unsuccessful at the Brand Expression layer regardless of outcomes at later gates.

| Gate | Evaluation discipline |
|------|----------------------|
| **1 — Constitutional** | Compliance with Volume 02 P1–P11; no contradiction with inherited architectural law |
| **2 — Layer 5** | Compliance with visual exclusions, accessibility foundations, and library-level quality foundations where implicated |
| **3 — Philosophical** | Apply frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001` — philosophical reconcilability required; Layer 1 evaluation discipline SHALL NOT be redefined here |
| **4 — Brand-appropriateness** | Compliance with identity-bound presentation authority and brand boundary discipline |
| **5 — Layer 2 scope** | The decision stays within Layer 2 authority and does not govern downstream principal subjects |

A treatment that passes brand-appropriateness but fails philosophical reconcilability is unsuccessful. A treatment that passes philosophical reconcilability but fails brand-appropriateness is unsuccessful. A treatment that passes both may still fail if it exceeds Layer 2 authority or violates an upstream gate.

#### Normative requirements — Stacked evaluation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R09` | Brand expression decisions SHALL be evaluated through the stacked evaluation procedure in Section 6.1. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R10` | Constitutional and Layer 5 gates SHALL be applied before brand preference or brand-appropriateness evaluation. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R11` | Philosophical reconcilability SHALL be evaluated by applying frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`; this standard SHALL NOT redefine Layer 1 evaluation architecture. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R12` | Brand-appropriateness SHALL be required for brand expression success in addition to, not instead of, philosophical reconcilability. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R13` | A brand expression decision SHALL fail stacked evaluation if it exceeds Layer 2 authority by governing element specifications, composition rules, surface layout, selection policy, collection operations, Brain behavior, or implementation requirements. | Company judgment |

### 6.2 Judgment procedure

Authors of brand expression rules and brand expression decisions SHALL apply the following judgment discipline.

#### Judgment procedure

1. **Confirm principal subject** — apply the identity-bound placement test (Section 5.2); confirm `CLS-BEX` ownership or defer to the correct layer or volume
2. **State the identity-bound question** — identify what brand identity or brand-consistent discipline is being decided
3. **Apply stacked evaluation** — execute Section 6.1 gates in order
4. **Record downstream impact** — identify which Layer 3, Layer 4, or peer volume standards are constrained
5. **Record reconciliation** — demonstrate reconcilability at freeze review per Section 6.4 where applicable

#### Enduring judgment principles

These principles express Layer 2 reasoning discipline. They do not author domain-specific rules or asset specifications.

| Principle | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| **Identity before element** | If the normative answer depends on F.I. Forgot brand identity, the decision is identity-bound — not a generic element rule |
| **Boundary before asset** | Brand expression governs what identity permits; it does not prescribe asset catalogs in this standard |
| **Constraint without absorption** | Layer 2 may bound downstream domains; it may not rewrite their principal subjects |

#### Normative requirements — Judgment procedure

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R14` | Every brand expression decision SHALL confirm `CLS-BEX` ownership through the identity-bound placement test before approval. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R15` | Every brand expression decision SHALL state the identity-bound presentation question being decided. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R16` | Every brand expression decision SHALL complete the judgment procedure in Section 6.2 — including principal-subject confirmation, identity-bound question statement, stacked evaluation, downstream impact identification, and reconciliation recording where applicable — before approval. | Company judgment |

### 6.3 Conflict reconciliation

Competing brand expression goals are permanent. Brand expression does not eliminate tension. It governs how tensions are resolved.

When normative brand expression goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the following procedure after identifying competing principal subjects per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1:

1. Apply Constitutional Inheritance — prohibited treatments remain prohibited regardless of brand preference
2. Apply Layer 5 authority where visual exclusion, accessibility foundations, or safety-related visual boundaries are implicated
3. Apply philosophical reconcilability per frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, including its conflict reconciliation procedure where philosophical goals are in tension
4. Apply brand-appropriateness and brand boundary discipline
5. If unresolved — escalate through governed revision, REG reconciliation, or architecture review. Silent domain override is prohibited.

No brand quality automatically wins every conflict without passing the stacked evaluation gates.

*Illustrative note (non-normative):* Authors may document specific tensions encountered during standard drafting. Such documentation does not create a permanent conflict taxonomy. Resolution follows the procedure above.

#### Normative requirements — Conflict reconciliation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R17` | When normative brand expression goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the conflict reconciliation procedure in Section 6.3 before approval. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R18` | Conflict resolution SHALL apply Layer 5 authority and philosophical reconcilability before brand-specific convenience. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R19` | No brand presentation quality SHALL automatically prevail in every conflict without stacked evaluation. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R20` | Unresolved brand expression conflicts SHALL NOT be settled by silent override. Governed escalation is required. | Company judgment |

### 6.4 Downstream reconciliation

Brand expression binds downstream standards. Downstream standards SHALL remain reconcilable with this standard when Layer 2 authority constrains their principal subjects.

Downstream reconciliation does not authorize Brand Expression to author downstream rules. It requires that downstream standards demonstrate consistency with identity-bound constraints at individual freeze review per `FI-DSN-GOV-001` §7.2.

When identity-bound rules in this standard change through governed revision per `FI-DSN-GOV-001` §15, affected downstream standards SHALL be assessed for reconcilability before dependent work is treated as complete.

#### Normative requirements — Downstream reconciliation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R21` | Future Volume 02 standards and peer-volume standards constrained by Layer 2 SHALL demonstrate reconcilability with this standard during individual freeze review. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-001-R22` | Governed revision to identity-bound rules in this standard SHALL trigger reconcilability assessment for materially affected downstream standards recorded in `FI-DSN-REG-001` **Dependencies**. | Company judgment |
