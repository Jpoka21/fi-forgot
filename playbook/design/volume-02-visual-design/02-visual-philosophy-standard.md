**Standard statement:** F.I. Forgot maintains **one authoritative Visual Philosophy** that defines why governed visuals exist, how visual success is evaluated at the philosophy layer, and how future visual standards judge and reconcile competing goals. This standard populates Layer 1 — Visual Intent. It does not define brand assets, element systems, composition rules, surface layout, artwork selection, collection operations, or implementation behavior.

**Source basis:** Company judgment per `FI-DSN-GOV-003`. This standard is not derived from vendor facts, verified evidence, or Brain runtime behavior.

## 1. Purpose

This standard is the **normative philosophical foundation** for F.I. Forgot Volume 02 Layer 1 — Visual Intent.

It translates approved Layer 1 architecture into enduring philosophical commitments and judgment discipline that downstream standards in Brand Expression, Element Systems, Composition, and Visual Boundaries must remain reconcilable with.

This standard does not replace frozen Volume 02 Visual Design Architecture. It operates within that architecture.

## 5. Purpose Architecture

F.I. Forgot is in the business of strengthening relationships. Governed visuals exist to serve that purpose — not to demonstrate design capability, novelty, or decorative skill for their own sake.

### 5.1 Why governed visuals exist

Governed visuals exist to **prepare, support, and amplify emotional communication** between sender and recipient. They create conditions in which a handwritten message can land with greater personal meaning for the recipient. They are **subordinate infrastructure** for the relationship moment the product delivers.

Governed visuals are not the product's primary value. The sender's intentional communication and the recipient's felt experience are.

### 5.2 Enduring philosophical commitments

The following commitments are enduring. They SHALL remain valid across branding refresh, artwork style change, new media, new manufacturers, AI-generated imagery, and future presentation technologies.

| Commitment | Enduring principle |
|------------|-------------------|
| **Relationship purpose** | Visual design exists to strengthen sender–recipient emotional connection |
| **Message centrality** | The handwritten message is the emotional centerpiece; visuals exist to support it |
| **Supportive presence** | Visuals prepare the recipient emotionally; they do not compete with the message |
| **Personal meaning** | Visuals should help the recipient experience the communication as meant for them, not as generic output |

*Explanatory rationale (non-normative):* F.I. Forgot intends that recipients remember the feeling of receiving the communication more than the mechanism that produced it. This rationale informs philosophy but does not create implementation, product, or technology obligations.

### 5.3 Normative requirements — Purpose Architecture

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R05` | Governed visuals SHALL exist to strengthen sender–recipient emotional connection. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R06` | Governed visuals SHALL support the handwritten message as the emotional centerpiece of the product experience, consistent with P11 Message Primacy. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R07` | Governed visuals SHALL NOT substitute for intentional message content or replace the sender's voice with decorative impact. | Company judgment |

| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R09` | Governed visuals SHALL support the recipient's experience of personal meaning — that the communication was meant for them specifically, not generically. | Company judgment |

## 6. Decision Architecture

### 6.1 Outcome evaluation philosophy

Visual decisions SHALL be evaluated through **outcomes**, not appearance in isolation.

**Relationship-centered evaluation primacy:** Visual success SHALL be judged first by whether a treatment strengthens sender–recipient emotional connection and supports the message's personal meaning for the recipient. A visually impressive treatment that weakens emotional communication or personal meaning is philosophically unsuccessful regardless of aesthetic merit.

Appearance and craft may inform judgment. They do **not** determine philosophical success alone.

#### Normative requirements — Outcome evaluation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R10` | Visual decisions SHALL be evaluated through outcomes, not visual appearance alone. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R11` | Relationship-centered evaluation SHALL be primary when judging whether a governed visual decision is philosophically successful. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R12` | A governed visual treatment that weakens emotional communication or the recipient's experience of personal meaning SHALL be considered philosophically unsuccessful regardless of aesthetic impressiveness. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R13` | Governed visual decisions SHALL NOT be justified by decorative impact, novelty, or trend appeal alone. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R14` | Added visual richness or complexity SHALL be justified by relationship outcome. | Company judgment |

### 6.2 Judgment principles

Future visual standards and visual decision authors SHALL apply the following judgment discipline when authoring or evaluating governed visual rules.

#### Judgment procedure

1. **State the principal subject** — identify what normative visual question is being decided
2. **State the relationship purpose** — identify which relationship outcome the decision serves
3. **State the emotional risk** — identify how the decision could weaken message support or the recipient's experience of personal meaning
4. **Evaluate against Purpose Architecture** — confirm consistency with Section 5 enduring commitments
5. **Evaluate against Constitutional Inheritance** — confirm consistency with Volume 02 P1–P11
6. **Record reconciliation** — downstream standards SHALL demonstrate reconcilability with this frozen standard at freeze review

#### Enduring judgment principles

These principles express Layer 1 reasoning discipline. They do not author domain-specific rules.

| Principle | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| **Purpose before ornament** | Visual ornament without stated relationship purpose fails philosophical review |
| **Anticipation before display** | Visuals should earn attention by preparing emotional readiness for the message, not by demanding attention for themselves |
| **Personal meaning before generic impression** | Treatments that read as generically produced, without supporting the recipient's sense of personal relevance, fail relationship evaluation |

#### Normative requirements — Judgment principles

|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R15` | Every governed visual decision SHALL identify the relationship outcome it serves. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R16` | Every governed visual decision SHALL identify the emotional risk it introduces to message support or the recipient's experience of personal meaning. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R17` | Future Volume 02 standards constrained by Layer 1 SHALL demonstrate reconcilability with this standard during individual freeze review per `FI-DSN-GOV-001` §7.2. | Company judgment |

### 6.3 Conflict reconciliation

Competing visual goals are permanent. Philosophy does not eliminate tension. It governs how tensions are resolved.

#### Conflict reconciliation procedure

When normative visual goals conflict:

1. Identify the competing principal subjects per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1
2. Apply Constitutional Inheritance — prohibited treatments remain prohibited regardless of philosophical preference
3. Apply relationship-centered evaluation primacy
4. Reconcile against enduring commitments in this standard (Section 5)
5. If the conflict implicates visual exclusion, accessibility, or safety — **Layer 5 authority prevails**
6. If unresolved — escalate through governed revision, REG reconciliation, or architecture review. Silent domain override is prohibited.

No visual quality automatically wins every conflict. Authors SHALL resolve tensions by determining which resolution better serves sender–recipient emotional connection and the recipient's experience of personal meaning.

*Illustrative note (non-normative):* Authors may document specific tensions encountered during standard drafting. Such documentation does not create a permanent conflict taxonomy. Resolution follows the procedure above.

#### Normative requirements — Conflict reconciliation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R18` | When normative visual goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the conflict reconciliation procedure in Section 6.3 before approving a governed visual decision. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R19` | Conflict resolution SHALL apply relationship-centered evaluation primacy after Constitutional Inheritance and before domain-specific convenience. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R20` | No visual quality SHALL automatically prevail in every conflict without relationship-centered evaluation. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R21` | When conflict implicates visual exclusion, accessibility foundations, or safety-related visual boundaries, Layer 5 authority SHALL prevail per `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R03`. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-PRN-001-R22` | Unresolved visual goal conflicts SHALL NOT be settled by silent override. Governed escalation is required. | Company judgment |
