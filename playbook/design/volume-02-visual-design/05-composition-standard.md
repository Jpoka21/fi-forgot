**Standard statement:** F.I. Forgot maintains **one authoritative Composition** standard that defines how composition-domain decisions are governed, how permitted relationships among visual elements are evaluated, and how Layer 4 composition operates within philosophical, brand, and element authority without absorbing element character, surface layout, handwritten message presentation, selection policy, or implementation behavior. This standard populates Layer 4 — Composition Principles for relational discipline under `CLS-CMP`. It does not define template regions, safe areas, coordinates, element specifications, hierarchy recipes, measurement systems, or engineering implementation.

**Source basis:** Company judgment per `FI-DSN-GOV-003`. This standard is not derived from vendor facts, verified evidence, or Brain runtime behavior.

---

## 1. Purpose

This standard is the **normative governing foundation** for F.I. Forgot Volume 02 Layer 4 — Composition Principles (`CLS-CMP` when relational).

It translates approved Layer 4 composition architecture into enduring composition-domain commitments and judgment discipline that downstream standards in Visual Boundaries, Volume 03 layout, and peer volumes must remain reconcilable with.

This standard does not replace frozen Volume 02 Visual Design Architecture, `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, `FI-DSN-STD-001`, or `FI-DSN-STD-002`. It operates within that architecture and remains subordinate to Layer 1 philosophical authority, Layer 2 brand authority, and Layer 3 element authority.

## 5. Purpose Architecture

Composition governs **permitted relationships among visual elements** as a domain-agnostic relational discipline within F.I. Forgot governed visuals. It defines what composition decisions belong to Layer 4 and what permanent relational discipline governs those decisions — without becoming a layout specification, element catalog, or implementation guide.

### 5.1 Composition-domain authority

A decision belongs to the composition domain when relational discipline among visual elements is the **principal normative subject**.

**Placement discipline** — authors SHALL apply the following test to determine whether `CLS-CMP` / Layer 4 is the appropriate principal classification for relational discipline. This test operationalizes `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1 and frozen Volume 02 §12.3; it does not replace principal-subject law.

| Step | Question | If yes → |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Is relational discipline among visual elements — hierarchy, balance, or prominence — the principal normative subject? | Candidate for `CLS-CMP` / Layer 4 Composition (relational) |
| 2 | Would the normative answer change if F.I. Forgot brand identity were removed from the decision? | Layer 2 — `CLS-BEX` when identity-bound expression is principal |
| 3 | Is a single element type's character or system behavior the principal subject? | Layer 3 — applicable `CLS-TYP`, `CLS-COL`, `CLS-ILL`, `CLS-PHO`, or `CLS-ART` |
| 4 | Is handwritten message presentation the principal subject? | Manufacturing / message-engine — not `CLS-CMP` |
| 5 | Is surface layout, template regions, or coordinates the principal subject? | Volume 03 — `CLS-CMP` when surface structure is principal |
| 6 | Is a global prohibition or accessibility minimum the principal subject regardless of compositional intent? | Layer 5 |

When steps conflict, **principal normative subject** per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1 governs.

Composition owns **relational discipline** among elements. It does not own element character, identity-bound brand presentation where `CLS-BEX` is principal, or surface placement per frozen Volume 02 §12.1.

*Illustrative note (non-normative):* Identity-level presentation posture belongs to Layer 2; relational presentation among elements belongs to Layer 4.

### 5.2 Composition-domain discipline

Composition-domain discipline governs three enduring relational facets as one constitutional subject:

| Facet | Governing role |
|-------|----------------|
| **Relational hierarchy** | Order and structural subordination among elements — which element leads, follows, or yields in relational structure |
| **Balance and visual weight** | Distribution of visual mass and equilibrium among elements, including whitespace and calm at principle level |
| **Prominence among elements** | Relative visual dominance and recession among elements — which elements may command or surrender attention in relationship to others |

Relational hierarchy governs **order and subordination**. Prominence among elements governs **dominance and recession**. A decision whose principal subject is structural order belongs to relational hierarchy; a decision whose principal subject is attention weight among coexisting elements belongs to prominence. Authors SHALL NOT treat these facets as interchangeable.

Composition-domain discipline is **not** brand boundary discipline. A relational rule may comply with composition-domain discipline while requiring separate brand and element reconcilability evaluation. Composition-domain discipline is **not** Layer 5 global exclusion and **not** surface layout.

## 6. Decision Architecture

### 6.1 Stacked evaluation procedure

Composition decisions SHALL be evaluated through a **stacked evaluation procedure**. Each gate is sequential. Failure at any gate renders the decision unsuccessful at the Composition layer regardless of outcomes at later gates.

| Gate | Evaluation discipline |
|------|----------------------|
| **1 — Constitutional** | Compliance with Volume 02 P1–P11; no contradiction with inherited architectural law |
| **2 — Layer 5** | Compliance with visual exclusions, accessibility foundations, and library-level quality foundations where implicated |
| **3 — Upstream reconcilability** | Apply frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001` (philosophical reconcilability), then frozen `FI-DSN-STD-001` (brand reconcilability), then frozen `FI-DSN-STD-002` (element reconcilability); upstream standards SHALL NOT be redefined here |
| **4 — Composition-appropriateness** | Compliance with composition-domain authority and composition-domain discipline |
| **5 — Layer 4 scope** | The decision stays within Layer 4 composition authority and does not govern element character, brand identity where `CLS-BEX` is principal, handwritten message presentation, surface layout, contextual selection policy, Brain behavior, or implementation |

A treatment that passes composition-appropriateness but fails upstream reconcilability is unsuccessful. A treatment that passes upstream reconcilability but fails composition-appropriateness is unsuccessful. A treatment that passes both may still fail if it exceeds Layer 4 authority or violates an upstream gate.

#### Normative requirements — Stacked evaluation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-003-R08` | Composition decisions SHALL be evaluated through the stacked evaluation procedure in Section 6.1. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-003-R09` | Constitutional and Layer 5 gates SHALL be applied before compositional preference or composition-appropriateness evaluation. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-003-R10` | Upstream reconcilability SHALL be evaluated by applying frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, then frozen `FI-DSN-STD-001`, then frozen `FI-DSN-STD-002`; this standard SHALL NOT redefine Layer 1, Layer 2, or Layer 3 evaluation architecture. | Company judgment |

### 6.2 Judgment procedure

Authors of composition rules and composition decisions SHALL apply the following judgment discipline.

#### Judgment procedure

1. **Confirm principal subject** — apply the placement test (Section 5.1); confirm Layer 4 relational ownership or defer to the correct layer or volume
2. **State the composition-domain question** — identify what relational treatment among elements is being decided
3. **Apply stacked evaluation** — execute Section 6.1 gates in order
4. **Record downstream impact** — identify which Layer 5, Volume 03, or peer volume standards are constrained
5. **Record reconcilability** — demonstrate reconcilability at freeze review per `FI-DSN-GOV-001` §7.2 and record material dependencies in `FI-DSN-REG-001` where applicable

#### Enduring judgment principles

These principles express Layer 4 composition reasoning discipline. They do not author domain-specific rules or layout specifications.

| Principle | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| **Relationships before elements** | If the normative answer depends on how elements relate to one another, the decision is composition-domain — not element, brand, or implementational |
| **Principles before placement** | Composition governs what relational treatments may exist; it does not govern where elements are placed on a surface |
| **Message presentation is out of scope** | Handwritten message presentation is not a composition-domain subject for this product |

#### Normative requirements — Judgment procedure

### 6.3 Conflict reconciliation

Competing composition goals are permanent. Composition governance does not eliminate tension. It governs how tensions are resolved.

When normative composition goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the following procedure after identifying competing principal subjects per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1:

1. Apply Constitutional Inheritance — prohibited treatments remain prohibited regardless of compositional preference
2. Apply Layer 5 authority where visual exclusion, accessibility foundations, or safety-related visual boundaries are implicated
3. Apply upstream reconcilability per frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, frozen `FI-DSN-STD-001`, and frozen `FI-DSN-STD-002`, including their conflict reconciliation procedures where upstream goals are in tension
4. Apply composition-appropriateness and composition-domain discipline
5. If unresolved — escalate through governed revision, REG reconciliation, or architecture review. Silent domain override is prohibited.

No compositional quality automatically wins every conflict without passing the stacked evaluation gates.

*Illustrative note (non-normative):* Authors may document specific tensions encountered during standard drafting. Such documentation does not create a permanent conflict taxonomy. Resolution follows the procedure above.

#### Normative requirements — Conflict reconciliation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |

| `FI-DSN-STD-003-R18` | No compositional quality SHALL automatically prevail in every conflict without stacked evaluation. | Company judgment |

## 7. Company Judgment

This standard is entirely based on company judgment.

F.I. Forgot has chosen to govern composition as a distinct Layer 4 relational domain because philosophical, brand, and element authority alone cannot determine what relationships among visual elements may exist as reusable visual discipline. That separation is not derived from vendor documentation, verified manufacturing facts, or Brain runtime behavior.

`FI-DSN-PRN-001`, `FI-DSN-STD-001`, and `FI-DSN-STD-002` informed drafting as frozen upstream authority. Layer 1, Layer 2, and Layer 3 requirements are applied, not re-authored, unless explicitly incorporated here as Layer 4 requirements.
