**Standard statement:** F.I. Forgot maintains **one authoritative Illustration and Artwork Direction** standard that defines how identity-level artwork character is governed, how artwork-domain decisions are evaluated, and how Layer 3 artwork direction operates within philosophical and brand authority without absorbing contextual selection, asset realization, surface layout, or implementation behavior. This standard populates Layer 3 — Visual Element Systems for cross-medium artwork character and identity-level artwork direction. It does not define which artwork applies in a given context, artwork ranking, generation workflows, prompt engineering, production-readiness approval, or any requirement that Dave imagery appear on card surfaces.

**Source basis:** Company judgment per `FI-DSN-GOV-003`. This standard is not derived from vendor facts, verified evidence, or Brain runtime behavior.

---

## 1. Purpose

This standard is the **normative governing foundation** for F.I. Forgot Volume 02 Layer 3 — Illustration and Artwork Direction (`CLS-ART`).

It translates approved Layer 3 artwork architecture into enduring artwork-domain commitments and judgment discipline that downstream standards in Composition, Visual Boundaries, Volume 04 contextual intelligence, and peer volumes must remain reconcilable with.

This standard does not replace frozen Volume 02 Visual Design Architecture, `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, or `FI-DSN-STD-001`. It operates within that architecture and remains subordinate to Layer 1 philosophical authority and Layer 2 brand authority.

## 5. Purpose Architecture

Artwork governs the **illustration and artwork element domain** as a reusable visual building block within F.I. Forgot governed visuals. It defines what artwork decisions belong to `CLS-ART` and what permanent discipline governs those decisions — without becoming an artwork selection standard, asset catalog, generation workflow, or manufacturing production guide.

### 5.1 Artwork-domain authority

A decision belongs to the artwork domain when illustration character, artwork character, artwork usage, or identity-level artwork direction is the **principal normative subject**.

**Placement discipline** — authors SHALL apply the following test to determine whether `CLS-ART` is the appropriate principal classification. This test operationalizes `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1; it does not replace principal-subject law.

| Step | Question | If yes → |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Is illustration character, artwork character, artwork usage, or identity-level artwork direction the principal normative subject? | Candidate for `CLS-ART` / Layer 3 Illustration and Artwork Direction |
| 2 | Would the normative answer change if F.I. Forgot brand identity were removed from the decision? | Layer 2 — `CLS-BEX` when identity-bound expression is principal |
| 3 | Is relational discipline among elements — hierarchy, balance, prominence, density — the principal subject? | Layer 4 — `CLS-CMP` when relational |
| 4 | Is handwritten message presentation the principal subject? | Manufacturing / message-engine — not `CLS-ART` |
| 5 | Is surface layout, template regions, or coordinates the principal subject? | Volume 03 |
| 6 | Is contextual artwork selection, occasion fit, personalization, or ranking the principal subject? | Volume 04 — `CLS-BVS` / `CLS-OEC` / `CLS-PER` when principal |
| 7 | Is a global prohibition or accessibility minimum the principal subject regardless of artwork intent? | Layer 5 |

When steps conflict, **principal normative subject** per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1 governs.

Artwork owns **identity-level artwork character and illustration direction** as an element domain. It does not own art-vs-text prominence, cross-element relational hierarchy, contextual selection, or typographic character per frozen Volume 02 §12.1.

### 5.2 Artwork-domain discipline

Artwork-domain discipline governs three enduring facets as one constitutional subject:

| Facet | Governing role |
|-------|----------------|
| **Artwork character** | What illustration and artwork treatment may be as a reusable element system — enduring style boundaries and character categories, not specific asset files or selection outcomes |
| **Illustration direction** | How identity-level illustration and artwork express governed visual character — treatment discipline and illustration boundaries within the artwork domain, not cross-element compositional hierarchy |
| **Element boundaries** | What artwork as an element domain may and may not do — permitted, required, and prohibited artwork treatment distinct from brand boundaries, Volume 04 contextual intelligence, and Layer 5 global exclusions |

Artwork-domain discipline is **not** brand boundary discipline. An artwork rule may comply with element-domain discipline while requiring separate brand reconcilability evaluation. Artwork-domain discipline is **not** Volume 04 contextual selection. Identity-level artwork law governs what artwork may be; Volume 04 governs which artwork applies in a given context.

## 6. Decision Architecture

### 6.1 Stacked evaluation procedure

Artwork decisions SHALL be evaluated through a **stacked evaluation procedure**. Each gate is sequential. Failure at any gate renders the decision unsuccessful at the Artwork layer regardless of outcomes at later gates.

| Gate | Evaluation discipline |
|------|----------------------|
| **1 — Constitutional** | Compliance with Volume 02 P1–P11; no contradiction with inherited architectural law |
| **2 — Layer 5** | Compliance with visual exclusions, accessibility foundations, and library-level quality foundations where implicated |
| **3 — Upstream reconcilability** | Apply frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001` (philosophical reconcilability), then frozen `FI-DSN-STD-001` (brand reconcilability); upstream standards SHALL NOT be redefined here |
| **4 — Artwork-appropriateness** | Compliance with artwork-domain authority and artwork-domain discipline |
| **5 — Layer 3 scope** | The decision stays within Layer 3 artwork authority and does not govern composition, typography, handwritten message, surface layout, contextual selection, artwork ranking, generation workflows, Brain behavior, or implementation |

A treatment that passes artwork-appropriateness but fails upstream reconcilability is unsuccessful. A treatment that passes upstream reconcilability but fails artwork-appropriateness is unsuccessful. A treatment that passes both may still fail if it exceeds Layer 3 authority or violates an upstream gate.

#### Normative requirements — Stacked evaluation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-017-R08` | Artwork decisions SHALL be evaluated through the stacked evaluation procedure in Section 6.1. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-017-R09` | Constitutional and Layer 5 gates SHALL be applied before artwork preference or artwork-appropriateness evaluation. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-017-R10` | Upstream reconcilability SHALL be evaluated by applying frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001` and then frozen `FI-DSN-STD-001`; this standard SHALL NOT redefine Layer 1 or Layer 2 evaluation architecture. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-017-R11` | Artwork-appropriateness SHALL be required for artwork success in addition to, not instead of, upstream reconcilability. | Company judgment |

An artwork decision that does so SHALL fail stacked evaluation at the Layer 3 scope gate. | Company judgment |

### 6.2 Judgment procedure

Authors of artwork rules and artwork decisions SHALL apply the following judgment discipline.

#### Judgment procedure

1. **Confirm principal subject** — apply the placement test (Section 5.1); confirm `CLS-ART` ownership or defer to the correct layer or volume
2. **State the artwork-domain question** — identify what artwork character, illustration direction, usage, or boundary is being decided
3. **Apply stacked evaluation** — execute Section 6.1 gates in order
4. **Record downstream impact** — identify which Layer 4, Volume 03, or peer volume standards are constrained
5. **Record reconcilability** — demonstrate reconcilability at freeze review per `FI-DSN-GOV-001` §7.2 and record material dependencies in `FI-DSN-REG-001` where applicable

#### Enduring judgment principles

These principles express Layer 3 artwork reasoning discipline. They do not author domain-specific rules or artwork specifications.

| Principle | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| **Character before selection** | If the normative answer depends on identity-level artwork character or illustration direction, the decision is artwork-domain — not Volume 04 contextual selection |
| **Direction before asset** | Artwork governs what illustration and artwork may be; it does not govern which asset file is chosen in a given context |
| **Identity before convenience** | Artwork governance governs decision discipline and character boundaries; it does not prescribe specific artwork assets or ranking heuristics |

#### Normative requirements — Judgment procedure

|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-017-R13` | Every artwork decision SHALL confirm `CLS-ART` ownership through the placement test before approval. | Company judgment |

### 6.3 Conflict reconciliation

Competing artwork goals are permanent. Artwork governance does not eliminate tension. It governs how tensions are resolved.

When normative artwork goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the following procedure after identifying competing principal subjects per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1:

1. Apply Constitutional Inheritance — prohibited treatments remain prohibited regardless of artwork preference
2. Apply Layer 5 authority where visual exclusion, accessibility foundations, or safety-related visual boundaries are implicated
3. Apply upstream reconcilability per frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001` and frozen `FI-DSN-STD-001`, including their conflict reconciliation procedures where upstream goals are in tension
4. Apply artwork-appropriateness and artwork-domain discipline
5. If unresolved — escalate through governed revision, REG reconciliation, or architecture review. Silent domain override is prohibited.

No artwork quality automatically wins every conflict without passing the stacked evaluation gates.

*Illustrative note (non-normative):* Authors may document specific tensions encountered during standard drafting. Such documentation does not create a permanent conflict taxonomy. Resolution follows the procedure above.

#### Normative requirements — Conflict reconciliation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-017-R16` | When normative artwork goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the conflict reconciliation procedure in Section 6.3 before approval. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-017-R17` | Conflict resolution SHALL apply Layer 5 authority and upstream reconcilability before artwork-specific convenience. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-017-R18` | No artwork quality SHALL automatically prevail in every conflict without stacked evaluation. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-017-R19` | Unresolved artwork conflicts SHALL NOT be settled by silent override. Governed escalation is required. | Company judgment |

## 7. Company Judgment

This standard is entirely based on company judgment.

F.I. Forgot has chosen to govern illustration and artwork direction as a distinct Layer 3 element domain because philosophical and brand authority alone cannot determine what artwork character may be as a reusable visual building block, and because identity-level artwork law must remain separable from Volume 04 contextual selection. That separation is not derived from vendor documentation, verified manufacturing facts, or Brain runtime behavior.

`FI-DSN-PRN-001` and `FI-DSN-STD-001` informed drafting as frozen upstream authority. Layer 1 and Layer 2 requirements are applied, not re-authored, unless explicitly incorporated here as Layer 3 requirements.
