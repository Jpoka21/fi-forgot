This standard is the **normative governing foundation** for F.I. Forgot Volume 03 Domain 2 — Surface Spatial Allocation under `CLS-CMP` surface-bound.

## 5. Purpose Architecture

Surface Spatial Allocation governs **greeting card surface spatial allocation** as the single permanent constitutional subject under `CLS-CMP` surface-bound. It defines what surface-spatial-allocation decisions belong to Domain 2 and what enduring spatial discipline governs those decisions — without becoming a coordinate specification, metadata schema, manufacturing integration standard, or implementation guide.

### 5.1 Surface-spatial-allocation-domain authority

A decision belongs to the surface-spatial-allocation domain when spatial allocation within established greeting card surface structure is the **principal normative subject**.

**Placement discipline** — authors SHALL apply the following test to determine whether `CLS-CMP` surface-bound / Domain 2 is the appropriate principal classification. This test operationalizes frozen Volume 03 §15, `FI-DSN-STD-004` §5.1, and `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1; it does not replace principal-subject law.

| Step | Question | If yes → |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Is spatial placement within an established governed surface or among established structural regions the principal normative subject? | Candidate `CLS-CMP` surface-bound / Domain 2 |
| 2 | Would removing spatial placement rules leave a structural or system standard? | Defer to `CLS-CAR` / Domain 1 — `FI-DSN-STD-004` |
| 3 | Is structural or system surface architecture of the greeting card product the principal normative subject? | `CLS-CAR` / Domain 1 |
| 4 | Is relational discipline among visual elements without surface binding the principal subject? | Volume 02 Layer 4 — `CLS-CMP` relational — `FI-DSN-STD-003` |
| 5 | Is envelope or exterior presentation the principal subject? | `CLS-EEP` |
| 6 | Is manufacturing integration or cross-domain feasibility reconciliation the principal subject? | `CLS-MFI` |

When steps conflict, **principal normative subject** per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1 governs.

Surface Spatial Allocation owns **how permitted content occupies established structure**. It does not own what surfaces or regions exist, relational composition philosophy, envelope presentation, or manufacturing integration governance.

### 5.2 Card Surface Spatial Allocation

Card surface spatial allocation is the **one permanent purpose facet** of this standard. It comprises three constitutional subdisciplines as one spatial discipline:

| Subdiscipline | Governing role |
|---------------|----------------|
| **Regional placement and occupancy** | How permitted content occupies established structural regions; inter-regional position; surface-bound alignment and surface-bound grouping categories; layout-variant spatial patterns where governed card system structure is unchanged |
| **Spatial boundaries** | Safe-area discipline, protected spatial zones, and surface-bound layout-boundary rules within established structure — design-side spatial limits informed by manufacturing Compliance Boundaries, without dimensional specification |
| **Bleed integration** | Design-standard spatial treatment of edge-extending content when edge-extent-permitted structural class applies — without bleed geometry, trim offsets, or vendor specifications |

Structural existence precedes spatial allocation. Structural variant type precedes layout variant. Edge-extent-permitted structural permission precedes bleed integration discipline.

*Illustrative domain split (non-normative):*

| Statement | Authority |
|-----------|-----------|
| Hero region exists | `FI-DSN-STD-004` — structural region existence |
| Hero region is associated with the front surface | `FI-DSN-STD-004` — structural surface association |
| Hero region appears above the message region | Surface Spatial Allocation — inter-regional position |
| Message content occupies the inside message region | Surface Spatial Allocation — regional occupancy |
| Handwriting must remain within a protected zone | Surface Spatial Allocation — spatial boundary |
| Edge-extent-permitted structural class exists | `FI-DSN-STD-004` — structural variant class |
| Edge-extending artwork is governed at the design-standard level | Surface Spatial Allocation — bleed integration |
| Bleed extends beyond trim by a measurement | Engineering / implementation — not this standard |

## 6. Decision Architecture

### 6.1 Stacked evaluation procedure

Surface-spatial-allocation decisions SHALL be evaluated through the **stacked gate procedure** defined in frozen Volume 03 §10.1. Gates are sequential. Failure at any gate renders the decision unsuccessful regardless of outcomes at later gates.

| Gate | Order | Evaluation discipline |
|------|-------|----------------------|
| **1 — Constitutional** | First | Compliance with Volume 03 P1–P10; compliance with frozen Volume 02 Visual Design Architecture P1–P11; no contradiction with inherited Volume 02 architectural law, frozen `FI-DSN-STD-004`, or Volume 03 purpose architecture |
| **2 — Layer 5 / upstream boundary** | Second | Compliance with Volume 02 Layer 5 exclusions, accessibility foundations, and visual quality foundations where implicated |
| **3 — Upstream reconcilability** | Third | Apply frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, then `FI-DSN-STD-001`, then `FI-DSN-STD-002`, then `FI-DSN-STD-003` as upstream visual discipline; upstream standards MUST NOT be redefined. `FI-DSN-STD-004` is NOT an upstream visual standard — it is consumed as structural prerequisite at Gates 1 and 5 |
| **4 — Manufacturing Compliance Boundary** | Fourth | Applicable `FI-MFG-*` obligations satisfied; manufacturing policy MUST NOT be restated |
| **5 — Domain appropriateness** | Fifth | Decision belongs to `CLS-CMP` surface-bound / Domain 2; structure-vs-spatial removal test applied; `FI-DSN-STD-004` structure consumed as prerequisite |
| **6 — Volume 03 scope** | Sixth | Decision does not govern surface system structure, relational composition philosophy, envelope presentation, manufacturing integration policy, metadata semantics, selection policy, collection operations, Brain behavior, or implementation |

A treatment that passes Gate 5 — Domain appropriateness but fails upstream reconcilability is unsuccessful. A treatment that passes upstream reconcilability but violates Volume 03 scope is unsuccessful.

### 6.2 Judgment procedure

Authors of surface-spatial-allocation rules and surface-spatial-allocation decisions SHALL apply the following judgment discipline. These steps are procedural support for stacked evaluation — not additional constitutional gates.

#### Judgment procedure

1. **Confirm principal subject** — apply the placement test (Section 5.1); confirm `CLS-CMP` surface-bound / Domain 2 ownership or defer to the correct classification, volume, or `FI-DSN-STD-004`
2. **Confirm structural prerequisite** — verify governed surfaces, regions, and applicable structural variant classes are established by `FI-DSN-STD-004` and consumed, not re-authored
3. **Identify subdiscipline** — determine which constitutional subdiscipline in Section 5.2 is implicated
4. **Apply structure-vs-spatial removal test** — if removing positional or measurement language eliminates the normative subject, defer to `FI-DSN-STD-004`; if removing structural existence language leaves spatial allocation, retain in Domain 2
5. **Validate deferrals** — confirm `FI-DSN-STD-003`, `CLS-EEP`, `CLS-MFI`, `FI-DSN-GOV-002`, Volume 04, Volume 05, and engineering subjects are not absorbed
6. **Apply stacked evaluation** — execute Section 6.1 gates in order
7. **Record downstream impact** — identify which Volume 04, Volume 05, or engineering artifacts are constrained
8. **Record reconcilability** — demonstrate reconcilability at freeze review per `FI-DSN-GOV-001` §7.2 and record material dependencies in `FI-DSN-REG-001` where applicable

#### Enduring judgment principles

These principles express Domain 2 surface-spatial-allocation reasoning discipline. They do not author domain-specific coordinate rules or implementation specifications.

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Structure before placement** | Governed structure from `FI-DSN-STD-004` precedes every spatial allocation decision |
| **Position after identity** | Inter-regional position applies only among established structural regions |
| **Relational permission before surface pattern** | Upstream relational discipline is implemented on surfaces, not re-authored |
| **Boundary before convenience** | Spatial boundaries and Compliance Boundaries constrain placement preference |
| **Permission before bleed treatment** | Edge-extent-permitted structural permission from `FI-DSN-STD-004` precedes bleed integration discipline |

### 6.3 Conflict reconciliation

Competing surface-spatial-allocation goals are permanent. Surface Spatial Allocation governance does not eliminate tension. It governs how tensions are resolved.

When normative surface-spatial-allocation goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the following procedure after identifying competing principal subjects per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1:

1. Apply Constitutional Inheritance — prohibited treatments remain prohibited regardless of spatial preference
2. Apply Layer 5 authority where visual exclusion, accessibility foundations, or safety-related visual boundaries are implicated
3. Apply upstream reconcilability per frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, `FI-DSN-STD-001`, `FI-DSN-STD-002`, and `FI-DSN-STD-003`, including their conflict reconciliation procedures where upstream goals are in tension
4. Apply structural prerequisite discipline — `FI-DSN-STD-004` structural obligations constrain spatial allocation
5. Apply Compliance Boundary inheritance per applicable frozen `FI-MFG-*`
6. Apply surface-spatial-allocation-appropriateness and card surface spatial allocation discipline
7. If unresolved — escalate through governed revision, REG reconciliation, or architecture review. Silent domain override is prohibited.

Conflict resolution within Volume 03 SHALL apply frozen Volume 03 §10.2: Domain 1 structural obligations constrain Domain 2 spatial decisions; principal normative subject governs when domains appear to conflict.

#### Normative requirements — Conflict reconciliation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-005-R23` | When normative surface-spatial-allocation goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the conflict reconciliation procedure in Section 6.3, respecting Layer 5 authority, upstream reconcilability, structural prerequisite discipline, and Compliance Boundary inheritance before spatial-allocation convenience, before approval. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-005-R24` | No spatial-allocation preference SHALL automatically prevail in every conflict without stacked evaluation. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-005-R25` | Unresolved surface-spatial-allocation conflicts SHALL NOT be settled by silent override. Governed escalation is required. | Company judgment |
