## 5. Purpose Architecture

Card Architecture governs **greeting card surface system structure** as the single permanent constitutional subject under `CLS-CAR`. It defines what structural card-system decisions belong to Domain 1 and what enduring structural discipline governs those decisions — without becoming a layout specification, metadata schema, manufacturing integration standard, or implementation guide.

### 5.1 Card-architecture-domain authority

A decision belongs to the card-architecture domain when greeting card surface system structure is the **principal normative subject**.

**Placement discipline** — authors SHALL apply the following test to determine whether `CLS-CAR` / Domain 1 is the appropriate principal classification. This test operationalizes frozen Volume 03 §15 and `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1; it does not replace principal-subject law.

| Step | Question | If yes → |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Is structural or system surface architecture of the greeting card product the principal normative subject? | Candidate `CLS-CAR` / Domain 1 |
| 2 | Would removing spatial placement rules leave a structural or system standard? | Confirms `CLS-CAR` over `CLS-CMP` surface-bound |
| 3 | Is spatial placement within a governed surface the principal subject? | Domain 2 — `CLS-CMP` surface-bound |
| 4 | Is envelope or exterior presentation the principal subject? | `CLS-EEP` |
| 5 | Is manufacturing integration or cross-domain feasibility reconciliation the principal subject? | `CLS-MFI` |
| 6 | Is relational composition without surface binding the principal subject? | Volume 02 Layer 4 — `CLS-CMP` relational |

When steps conflict, **principal normative subject** per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1 governs.

Card Architecture owns **what the greeting card system structurally is**. It does not own spatial allocation on surfaces, envelope presentation, or manufacturing integration governance.

### 5.2 Card Surface System Structure

Card surface system structure is the **one permanent purpose facet** of this standard. It comprises five constitutional subdisciplines as one structural discipline:

| Subdiscipline | Governing role |
|---------------|----------------|
| **Card product topology** | Governed card surfaces; panel and fold topology; structural relationships among card surfaces; envelope as optional structural node; card-to-envelope containment and structural compatibility |
| **Template system structure** | How the governed template system is organized as structural architecture — template families and structural template types |
| **Structural regions** | Which governed content slots exist; stable region identity and type; structural surface or panel association |
| **Structural variants** | Which structural variant types change governed card system structure — including edge-extent-permitted structural class |
| **Production structure** | Design-side structural production form the governed card system embodies — fold type, surface set, and structural production assumptions |

Structural existence precedes spatial allocation. Structural variant type precedes layout variant. Production structure is part of what the card system structurally is — not a separate handoff facet.

*Illustrative domain split (non-normative):*

| Statement | Authority |
|-----------|-----------|
| Hero region exists | Domain 1 — structural region existence |
| Hero region is associated with the front surface | Domain 1 — structural surface association |
| Hero region appears above the message region | Domain 2 — spatial allocation |
| Edge-extent-permitted structural class exists | Domain 1 — structural variant class |
| Bleed extends beyond trim by a measurement | Domain 2 — bleed geometry |

## 6. Decision Architecture

### 6.1 Stacked evaluation procedure

Card-architecture decisions SHALL be evaluated through the **stacked gate procedure** defined in frozen Volume 03 §10.1. Gates are sequential. Failure at any gate renders the decision unsuccessful regardless of outcomes at later gates.

| Gate | Order | Evaluation discipline |
|------|-------|----------------------|
| **1 — Constitutional** | First | Compliance with Volume 03 P1–P10; compliance with frozen Volume 02 Visual Design Architecture P1–P11; no contradiction with inherited Volume 02 architectural law or Volume 03 purpose architecture |
| **2 — Layer 5 / upstream boundary** | Second | Compliance with Volume 02 Layer 5 exclusions, accessibility foundations, and visual quality foundations where implicated |
| **3 — Upstream reconcilability** | Third | Apply frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, then `FI-DSN-STD-001`, then `FI-DSN-STD-002`, then `FI-DSN-STD-003`; upstream standards MUST NOT be redefined |
| **4 — Manufacturing Compliance Boundary** | Fourth | Applicable `FI-MFG-*` obligations satisfied; manufacturing policy MUST NOT be restated |
| **5 — Domain appropriateness** | Fifth | Decision belongs to `CLS-CAR` / Domain 1; structure-vs-spatial removal test applied |
| **6 — Volume 03 scope** | Sixth | Decision does not govern element character, relational composition philosophy, spatial allocation, envelope presentation, manufacturing integration policy, selection policy, collection operations, Brain behavior, or implementation |

A treatment that passes Gate 5 — Domain appropriateness but fails upstream reconcilability is unsuccessful. A treatment that passes upstream reconcilability but violates Volume 03 scope is unsuccessful.

### 6.2 Judgment procedure

Authors of card-architecture rules and card-architecture decisions SHALL apply the following judgment discipline. These steps are procedural support for stacked evaluation — not additional constitutional gates.

#### Judgment procedure

1. **Confirm principal subject** — apply the placement test (Section 5.1); confirm `CLS-CAR` / Domain 1 ownership or defer to the correct classification or volume
2. **Identify subdiscipline** — determine which constitutional subdiscipline in Section 5.2 is implicated
3. **Apply structure-vs-spatial removal test** — if removing positional or measurement language eliminates the normative subject, defer to Domain 2
4. **Validate deferrals** — confirm `CLS-EEP`, `CLS-MFI`, `FI-DSN-GOV-002`, Volume 04, Volume 05, and engineering subjects are not absorbed
5. **Apply stacked evaluation** — execute Section 6.1 gates in order
6. **Record downstream impact** — identify which Domain 2, `CLS-EEP`, `CLS-MFI`, Volume 04, Volume 05, or engineering artifacts are constrained
7. **Record reconcilability** — demonstrate reconcilability at freeze review per `FI-DSN-GOV-001` §7.2 and record material dependencies in `FI-DSN-REG-001` where applicable

#### Enduring judgment principles

These principles express Domain 1 card-architecture reasoning discipline. They do not author domain-specific spatial rules or implementation specifications.

| Principle | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| **Structure before placement** | Existence and topology precede coordinates and positional relationships |
| **Identity before position** | Region types and identities precede spatial allocation |
| **Variant type before layout variant** | Structural variant class precedes layout differences within fixed structure |
| **Declarability is obligation, not schema** | Governed structure must be capable of declaration; GOV-002 owns semantics |
| **Compliance Boundary consumes, does not author** | Manufacturing facts cite `FI-MFG-*`; Card Architecture does not write manufacturing policy |

#### Normative requirements — Judgment procedure

|--------|-------------|--------|

### 6.3 Conflict reconciliation

Competing card-architecture goals are permanent. Card Architecture governance does not eliminate tension. It governs how tensions are resolved.

When normative card-architecture goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the following procedure after identifying competing principal subjects per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1:

1. Apply Constitutional Inheritance — prohibited treatments remain prohibited regardless of structural preference
2. Apply Layer 5 authority where visual exclusion, accessibility foundations, or safety-related visual boundaries are implicated
3. Apply upstream reconcilability per frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, `FI-DSN-STD-001`, `FI-DSN-STD-002`, and `FI-DSN-STD-003`, including their conflict reconciliation procedures where upstream goals are in tension
4. Apply Compliance Boundary inheritance per applicable frozen `FI-MFG-*`
5. Apply card-architecture-appropriateness and card surface system structure discipline
6. If unresolved — escalate through governed revision, REG reconciliation, or architecture review. Silent domain override is prohibited.

Conflict resolution within Volume 03 SHALL apply frozen Volume 03 §10.2: Domain 1 structural obligations constrain Domain 2 spatial decisions; principal normative subject governs when domains appear to conflict.

#### Normative requirements — Conflict reconciliation

| Req ID | Requirement | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| `FI-DSN-STD-004-R24` | When normative card-architecture goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the conflict reconciliation procedure in Section 6.3, respecting Layer 5 authority, upstream reconcilability, and Compliance Boundary inheritance before card-architecture convenience, before approval. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-004-R25` | No structural preference SHALL automatically prevail in every conflict without stacked evaluation. | Company judgment |
| `FI-DSN-STD-004-R26` | Unresolved card-architecture conflicts SHALL NOT be settled by silent override. Governed escalation is required. | Company judgment |
