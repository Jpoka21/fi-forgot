## 5. Purpose Architecture

Envelope and Exterior Presentation governs **envelope and exterior presentation** as the single permanent constitutional subject under `CLS-EEP`. It defines what envelope-and-exterior-presentation decisions belong to `CLS-EEP` and what enduring presentation discipline governs those decisions — without becoming a coordinate specification, metadata schema, manufacturing integration standard, or implementation guide.

### 5.1 Envelope-and-exterior-presentation-domain authority

A decision belongs to the envelope-and-exterior-presentation domain when envelope, exterior, or outward-facing presentation boundaries outside card structure and card-surface domains are the **principal normative subject**.

**Placement discipline** — authors SHALL apply the following test to determine whether `CLS-EEP` is the appropriate principal classification. This test operationalizes frozen Volume 03 §14.4, `FI-DSN-STD-004` §5.1, `FI-DSN-CLS-001` §10, and `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1; it does not replace principal-subject law.

| Step | Question | If yes → |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Is envelope, exterior, or outward-facing presentation outside card structure and card-surface domains the principal normative subject? | Candidate `CLS-EEP` |
| 2 | Would removing presentation rules leave a structural or card-surface spatial standard? | Defer to `CLS-CAR` / `FI-DSN-STD-004` or `CLS-CMP` surface-bound / `FI-DSN-STD-005` |
| 3 | Is greeting card surface system structure or envelope structural existence the principal subject? | `CLS-CAR` / `FI-DSN-STD-004` |
| 4 | Is spatial allocation within established greeting card surface structure the principal subject? | `CLS-CMP` surface-bound / `FI-DSN-STD-005` |
| 5 | Is brand identity where `CLS-BEX` is principal the normative subject? | Volume 02 Layer 2 — `FI-DSN-STD-001` |
| 6 | Is relational discipline among visual elements without exterior-surface binding the principal subject? | Volume 02 Layer 4 — `CLS-CMP` relational — `FI-DSN-STD-003` |
| 7 | Is manufacturing integration or cross-domain feasibility reconciliation the principal subject? | `CLS-MFI` |

When steps conflict, **principal normative subject** per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1 governs.

Envelope and Exterior Presentation owns **how outward-facing surfaces present** outside card structure and card-surface spatial allocation domains. It does not own what card surfaces or regions exist, how permitted content occupies card surfaces, brand identity itself, or manufacturing integration governance.

### 5.2 Envelope and Exterior Presentation

Envelope and exterior presentation is the **one permanent purpose facet** of this standard. It comprises two constitutional subdisciplines as one presentation discipline:

| Subdiscipline | Governing role |
|---------------|----------------|
| **Exterior and envelope surface presentation** | Envelope surface topology for presentation; exterior presentation framework on outward-facing surfaces; exterior orientation; reveal presentation visibility boundaries |
| **Exterior identity and communication presentation** | Address, recipient, and return-address presentation boundaries; branding presentation, logo presentation, exterior messaging presentation boundaries, seals at design treatment level, and outward-facing identity application boundaries — reconcilable with `FI-DSN-STD-001` and `FI-DSN-STD-003` within exterior bounds |

Structural prerequisite precedes exterior presentation. Exterior surface framework precedes exterior content presentation. Presentation-boundary treatment applies across both subdisciplines.

*Illustrative domain split (non-normative):*

| Statement | Authority |
|-----------|-----------|
| Envelope exists in product system | `FI-DSN-STD-004` — structural node |
| Card fits inside envelope structurally | `FI-DSN-STD-004` — containment |
| Which exterior zones may carry presentation | Envelope and Exterior Presentation — surface presentation |
| Where address may appear on exterior | Envelope and Exterior Presentation — identity and communication |
| Brand identity rules | `FI-DSN-STD-001` — reconciled on exterior |
| Hero region appears above message region on card | `FI-DSN-STD-005` — card-surface spatial allocation |
| Whether handwritten addressing is assumed | **Prohibited** — `FI-MFG-CON-001` Compliance Boundary |
| Address field pixel positions | Engineering / implementation — not this standard |

## 6. Decision Architecture

### 6.1 Stacked evaluation procedure

Envelope-and-exterior-presentation decisions SHALL be evaluated through the **stacked gate procedure** defined in frozen Volume 03 §10.1. Gates are sequential. Failure at any gate renders the decision unsuccessful regardless of outcomes at later gates.

| Gate | Order | Evaluation discipline |
|------|-------|----------------------|
| **1 — Constitutional** | First | Compliance with Volume 03 P1–P10; compliance with frozen Volume 02 Visual Design Architecture P1–P11; no contradiction with inherited Volume 02 architectural law, frozen `FI-DSN-STD-004`, frozen `FI-DSN-STD-005`, or Volume 03 purpose architecture |
| **2 — Layer 5 / upstream boundary** | Second | Compliance with Volume 02 Layer 5 exclusions, accessibility foundations, and visual quality foundations where implicated |
| **3 — Upstream reconcilability** | Third | Apply frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, then `FI-DSN-STD-001`, then `FI-DSN-STD-002`, then `FI-DSN-STD-003` as upstream visual discipline; upstream standards MUST NOT be redefined. `FI-DSN-STD-004` is NOT an upstream visual standard — it is consumed as structural prerequisite at Gates 1 and 5 |
| **4 — Manufacturing Compliance Boundary** | Fourth | Applicable `FI-MFG-*` obligations satisfied; manufacturing policy MUST NOT be restated |
| **5 — Domain appropriateness** | Fifth | Decision belongs to `CLS-EEP`; structure-vs-presentation removal test applied; `FI-DSN-STD-004` structure consumed as prerequisite; card-surface spatial allocation not absorbed |
| **6 — Volume 03 scope** | Sixth | Decision does not govern card surface system structure, card-surface spatial allocation, brand identity itself, relational composition philosophy, message content, copywriting, marketing strategy, communication strategy, addressing methods, postal compliance, shipping-label fulfillment, manufacturing integration policy, metadata semantics, contextual selection, artwork selection, personalization policy, collection operations, customer experience choreography, Brain behavior, or implementation |

A treatment that passes Gate 5 — Domain appropriateness but fails upstream reconcilability is unsuccessful. A treatment that passes upstream reconcilability but violates Volume 03 scope is unsuccessful.

### 6.2 Judgment procedure

Authors of envelope-and-exterior-presentation rules and envelope-and-exterior-presentation decisions SHALL apply the following judgment discipline. These steps are procedural support for stacked evaluation — not additional constitutional gates.

#### Judgment procedure

1. **Confirm principal subject** — apply the placement test (Section 5.1); confirm `CLS-EEP` ownership or defer to the correct classification, volume, `FI-DSN-STD-004`, or `FI-DSN-STD-005`
2. **Confirm structural prerequisite** — verify envelope structural facts established by `FI-DSN-STD-004` are consumed, not re-authored
3. **Identify subdiscipline** — determine which constitutional subdiscipline in Section 5.2 is implicated
4. **Apply structure-vs-presentation removal test** — if removing presentation language eliminates the normative subject, defer to `FI-DSN-STD-004` or `FI-DSN-STD-005`; if removing structural existence or card-surface spatial allocation language leaves exterior presentation, retain in `CLS-EEP`
5. **Validate deferrals** — confirm `FI-DSN-STD-001`, `FI-DSN-STD-003`, `FI-DSN-STD-005`, `CLS-MFI`, `FI-DSN-GOV-002`, Volume 04, Volume 05, and engineering subjects are not absorbed
6. **Apply stacked evaluation** — execute Section 6.1 gates in order
7. **Record downstream impact** — identify which Volume 04, Volume 05, or engineering artifacts are constrained
8. **Record reconcilability** — demonstrate reconcilability at freeze review per `FI-DSN-GOV-001` §7.2 and record material dependencies in `FI-DSN-REG-001` where applicable

### 6.3 Conflict reconciliation

Competing envelope-and-exterior-presentation goals are permanent. Envelope and Exterior Presentation governance does not eliminate tension. It governs how tensions are resolved.

When normative envelope-and-exterior-presentation goals conflict, authors SHALL apply the following procedure after identifying competing principal subjects per `FI-DSN-VOL-001` §14.1:

1. Apply Constitutional Inheritance — prohibited treatments remain prohibited regardless of exterior presentation preference
2. Apply Layer 5 authority where visual exclusion, accessibility foundations, or safety-related visual boundaries are implicated
3. Apply upstream reconcilability per frozen `FI-DSN-PRN-001`, `FI-DSN-STD-001`, `FI-DSN-STD-002`, and `FI-DSN-STD-003`, including their conflict reconciliation procedures where upstream goals are in tension
4. Apply structural prerequisite discipline — `FI-DSN-STD-004` structural obligations constrain exterior presentation
5. Apply Compliance Boundary inheritance per applicable frozen `FI-MFG-*`
6. Apply envelope-and-exterior-presentation-appropriateness and envelope and exterior presentation discipline
7. If unresolved — escalate through governed revision, REG reconciliation, or architecture review. Silent domain override is prohibited.

Conflict resolution within Volume 03 SHALL apply frozen Volume 03 §10.2: structural obligations constrain presentation decisions where implicated; principal normative subject governs when domains appear to conflict.
