# Read This Before You Change Anything

Welcome to F.I. Forgot.

This playbook is the source of truth for the company.

The code explains how the software works.

This playbook explains why the company exists.

Before changing the product, AI, marketing, copy, or design, read these documents.

Do not assume.

Do not copy trends.

Do not redesign simply because something looks modern.

Understand the philosophy first.

If your recommendation conflicts with the playbook, explain why before implementing it.

The goal is not to preserve old ideas forever.

The goal is to evolve intentionally.

When in doubt, remember this:

People don't need another app to manage.

They need one less thing to manage.
