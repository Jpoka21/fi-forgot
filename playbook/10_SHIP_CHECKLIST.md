# 10_SHIP_CHECKLIST.md

# Ship Checklist

Every meaningful feature, screen, workflow, redesign, or user experience change must pass this checklist before it is considered complete.

If the answer to any applicable question is **No**, continue iterating before shipping.

---

# Mission

□ Does this align with the Constitution?

□ Does this strengthen relationships?

□ Does this reduce customer effort?

□ Does this increase customer confidence?

□ Does this support our promise that important people never accidentally feel forgotten?

---

# Experience

□ Is there one obvious primary action?

□ Does every pixel earn its place?

□ Does every word earn its place?

□ Does the interface feel warm?

□ Does it feel premium?

□ Does it feel calm?

□ Does it make the service feel quieter instead of busier?

---

# Simplicity

□ Can Grandma understand this without instructions?

□ Can someone complete this while distracted?

□ Does this remove unnecessary decisions?

□ Could the system do more work instead of the customer?

□ Can anything be removed without reducing value?

---

# Brand

□ Does this feel like a trusted relationship service instead of software?

□ Is AI invisible to the customer?

□ Does it avoid looking like a CRM, dashboard, or enterprise application?

□ Does it sound like someone dependable has the customer's back?

---

# Communication

□ Is the copy clear?

□ Is the copy warm?

□ Does the customer immediately know what happens next?

□ Does the copy reduce uncertainty?

□ Does every message sound human?

---

# Final Judgment

□ Would Apple simplify this?

□ Would Amazon remove a step?

□ Would Dollar Shave Club make this feel more human?

□ If this were the first screen a new customer saw, would we be proud of it?

□ If we removed one more thing, would the experience actually improve?

---

# Before Shipping

Cursor should not declare work complete until this checklist has been reviewed.

If a tradeoff is necessary, explicitly explain:

* Which checklist item is being compromised.
* Why it is necessary.
* Why the chosen solution is still the best decision.

The playbook is the source of truth.

When in doubt, simplify.
