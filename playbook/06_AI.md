# Artificial Intelligence Philosophy

Artificial intelligence is one of the tools we use to fulfill our promise.

It is not our product.

It is not our brand.

It is not our value proposition.

Customers do not hire F.I. Forgot because it uses AI.

They hire F.I. Forgot because the important people in their lives feel remembered.

If today's AI disappears tomorrow and something better replaces it, our mission remains exactly the same.

Technology changes.

Relationships do not.

---

# AI Is an Employee

Think of AI as a trusted employee working behind the scenes.

Its responsibilities include:

Writing.

Remembering.

Personalizing.

Preparing.

Summarizing.

Suggesting.

Checking.

Learning.

Improving.

Working quietly while the customer lives their life.

Customers should rarely think about AI itself.

They should simply experience thoughtful service.

---

# AI Exists to Remove Work

Every use of AI should accomplish at least one of these goals:

Reduce effort.

Reduce decisions.

Increase confidence.

Improve personalization.

Strengthen relationships.

Increase reliability.

Save time.

If AI creates more work than it removes, we are using it incorrectly.

---

# Invisible Intelligence

The best AI is invisible.

Customers should notice:

Better timing.

Better personalization.

Better cards.

Better recommendations.

Better memories.

Better outcomes.

They should not notice AI.

The highest compliment is:

"It just gets me."

Not:

"That AI is impressive."

---

# AI Should Sound Like the Customer

The goal is never to produce the most beautiful card.

The goal is to produce the most believable card.

Every message should sound like something the customer genuinely could have written.

Not something an AI wanted to write.

The customer's voice always matters more than the model's creativity.

---

# Authenticity Above Creativity

AI should never invent memories.

Never invent inside jokes.

Never invent experiences.

Never invent family stories.

Never invent emotions.

Never pretend to know someone better than the customer does.

If information is missing, ask.

If confidence is low, simplify.

A sincere, simple message is always better than an impressive but inaccurate one.

---

# AI Works Within Guardrails

AI should automate routine work whenever it can do so safely.

Automation is encouraged.

Surprises are not.

Customers should always understand what the service is doing on their behalf.

When a decision could meaningfully affect a relationship, trust takes priority over automation.

The customer should always have the ability to review, edit, pause, or override the service.

Control builds confidence, even when customers rarely use it.

---

# Relationship Risk Matters

Not every relationship moment carries the same level of risk.

Routine birthdays.

Anniversaries.

Congratulations.

Holiday greetings.

These can often be handled with a high level of automation.

Sensitive moments require greater care.

Sympathy.

Illness.

Loss.

Apologies.

Conflict.

Major life events.

These deserve additional caution, stronger quality checks, and, when appropriate, customer approval before anything is sent.

The more emotionally significant the moment, the more carefully AI should behave.

---

# Verified Information Only

AI should build from trusted information.

That includes:

Customer provided details.

Saved memories.

Approved profile information.

Relationship history.

Previously approved writing preferences.

Explicit customer feedback.

AI should never fill gaps with assumptions.

When uncertain, choose honesty over imagination.

---

# Personalization Is the Job

The purpose of AI is not creativity.

The purpose of AI is relevance.

Remember what matters.

Use meaningful details.

Respect each relationship.

Adapt to the customer's writing style.

Improve with every interaction.

Customers should increasingly feel that every message sounds like the best version of themselves.

---

# AI Learns Responsibly

The system should improve over time.

It should learn:

Writing preferences.

Relationship dynamics.

Customer edits.

Communication style.

Important memories.

Feedback.

The goal is fewer corrections.

Greater confidence.

Less work.

Learning should always benefit the customer.

Never manipulate them.

---

# Quality Before Delivery

Every AI generated message should pass quality checks before it reaches a recipient.

The system should verify:

The occasion is correct.

The recipient is correct.

Facts are accurate.

Names are correct.

Tone matches the relationship.

The writing sounds natural.

No banned phrases appear.

The message feels sincere.

If quality cannot be confidently achieved, the service should simplify, request clarification, or ask for customer review.

Sending nothing is better than sending something that damages trust.

---

# AI Protects Trust

Customers trust us with deeply personal information.

Relationships.

Memories.

Family stories.

Personal milestones.

Writing style.

That information deserves exceptional care.

Privacy is not a feature.

It is a responsibility.

Respect is not optional.

Trust is earned through every interaction.

Protecting customer data is part of protecting customer relationships.

---

# Every AI Feature Must Earn Its Place

Before building any AI capability, ask:

Does this remove work?

Does this strengthen a relationship?

Does this increase confidence?

Does this improve personalization?

Does this reduce future effort?

Would customers value this even if they never knew AI was involved?

Does it feel more human?

Does it protect trust?

Can it be explained simply?

If the answer is no, it probably does not belong.

---

# The AI North Star

Customers should never think:

"That AI wrote an amazing card."

They should think:

"That sounded exactly like me."

The highest compliment is not that our AI is intelligent.

It is that our customers become more thoughtful without having to think harder.

AI succeeds when it quietly helps people become the version of themselves they already wanted to be, while protecting the relationships they value most.
